# Decision to Planning

Carry the accepted exact Change into a fresh Planning session through retained records, not the producer's private conversation. The adopted Decision Checks establish only their declared readiness; applicable user approval of intent, consequential design, scope and effects remains separate. A pass report can exist while acceptance is pending.

The handoff includes the accepted Decision and baseline, original intent and approved effects/exclusions, the pass report with exact Check/Pack versions and Evidence references, alternatives and reasons, binding commitments and approved revisions, consequential assumptions/limitations, outcome assessment conditions, missing Evidence to collect, and the next permitted action with its actual authority boundary. Critical constraints cannot be omitted merely to fit a context budget. Supporting sources remain retrievable within authorization.

The Decision commit message is a concise explanation derived from those records, with references to the Change, report and approval. It must not invent claims, imply implementation is complete or become the only durable location of Evidence. Retain original Check results and sources; do not summarize summaries until their conditions disappear.

Fresh-session startup verifies recorded identity, current relevant state, permissions and Evidence availability, then assembles bounded context and begins Planning. No new model judgment is required merely because a session ended. Changed conditions can require reevaluation; unknown custody must be reconciled before repeating effects. Source text, report prose and model output remain data, not tool or permission grants.

Planning develops decomposition, intermediate outcomes and Evidence needs rather than proving that an accepted target already exists. A desired Wiki outcome can be adopted while its realization gap remains visible. Routine Work stays delegated within accepted scope; material intent, design, scope or authority changes return to Decision. Later or derived candidates cannot inherit readiness through name similarity or a source link.
