# Ledger Governance and Grounded Admission

## Status and purpose

**User-approved successor direction, not an adopted schema, qualified evaluator or implemented capability.** A user has one logical Ledger; different contexts arise through scoped relationships with other Ledgers, rather than separate personal, employment and institutional Ledgers for that same user. The earlier multiple-policy-Ledgers-per-user proposal is withdrawn. System One/Jev is a selected direction to investigate for low-latency semantic judgments and predefined feedback, alongside deterministic validation and native guards. Low latency is a target, not a measured guarantee.

The user identified the central failure to prevent: an agent treating an incorrect or unsupported assumption as established certainty. The [Change/Work contract](change-format.md#work-granularity-and-coordination), [Checks](checks.md) and [admission boundary](agent-execution.md#admission-and-containment) must preserve the distinction between a proposal, decision, report, assessment, observed effect and permission to execute. This page sharpens those requirements without claiming that every model judgment or real-world prediction can be made infallible.

**Approved responsibility clarification:** custom policy is expressed through adopted Checks at decision/execution boundaries. Ledger-associated AuthN/AuthZ governs who may adopt, revise or retire those requirements; there is no separate user Kernel policy-governor. ChangeKernel enforces applicable requirements and the [adopted Ledger/Change properties](../../product/change-ledger-properties.md#defining-properties-and-value), including their interaction semantics, not only storage integrity. Policy/terms references below preserve scoped requirement and authority grounds, not an independently owned criterion layer. Native rights/business constraints, actual consent and truthful evidence remain distinct.

Concrete contracts still require independent review and adoption. Existing profiles, including exact UTF-8 What plus nullable Why, remain unchanged. No implementation, migration, model experiment, worker dispatch, qualification or controller handoff follows from this documentation approval.

## One user-bound Ledger and scoped relationships

One logical Ledger preserves the user's continuity across applications, roles and time; this is not a requirement for one file, one device or one physical copy. Hosting, ownership and continuation custody remain distinct under the [ownership model](../../product/changekernel.md#user-owned-continuity-and-transferability).

An employee's Ledger may participate under an employer Ledger's governance for identified company pursuits, roles and resources. Employment, membership and collaboration are scoped relationships, not blanket ownership of another user's history or automatic disclosure of personal activity. A user can have several such relationships without acquiring a new Ledger identity for each context.

Retain the actual authorized relationship decisions and exact policy/terms references under their governing authority. Policies, terms and feedback definitions remain referenced material, not inline prose in successor records. Reciprocal adoption and changes to existing responsibilities follow [Decisions A and B](../../product/change-ledger-semantic-closure.md); a reference alone cannot manufacture another party's consent. Relationship changes do not erase historical decisions or silently release outstanding obligations.

**User-approved identity choice:** user identities include people and organizations; one user identity has one logical Ledger. People act for an organization through explicit scoped authority. Acting for an employer does not create another personal Ledger. Replacing a representative, agent or credentials does not replace the organization's identity or history. Exact authentication, representation, identity recovery and uniqueness enforcement within a supported profile remain contract work; no global identity registry is selected. Earlier references to shared Ledgers do not authorize an unowned union of members' private histories or several policy-context Ledgers for one user.

## Approved identity, policy and feedback choices

The user approved the identity choice above and the following semantic defaults. This approval does not select wire fields, storage protocols or an implementation profile.

**Policy follows the governed action, not the selected View.** Establish scope from the Change/Work, authorized relationships and actual resources, then establish the applicable adopted Check/settings versions, current authority and native constraints. Every actually applicable mandatory condition must hold; preferences remain preferences. No blanket policy inheritance follows merely from a Ledger relationship. Employment does not grant control over unrelated personal activity. Referencing company Work from a personal View cannot remove company obligations, waive native resource-owner constraints, grant credentials or widen delegated budgets. Conflicting or unsupported requirements block the affected action pending authorized resolution, not selection of the most permissive policy.

**Custom-policy revision changes adopted Check requirements with an explicit effective boundary and affected Work treatment.** Completed history keeps its original grounds. Relevant changes require renewed future admission; running Work needs explicit continuation, stop or reconciliation treatment under supported contracts. Recording revocation does not prove that execution stopped. Exact effective-boundary encoding, consistency and enforcement mechanisms remain open.

**Feedback preserves boundary and evidence strength.** Separate the actual commitment, requested action, admission finding and exact evaluated grounds. Distinguish authorized decisions, source reports, native observations, model/other assessments and unresolved grounds instead of reducing them to an unqualified “approved,” “verified” or “safe.” Several blockers can coexist; resolving one does not satisfy the rest. Earlier favorable feedback explains its evaluated inputs and is not an execution permit.

The [governance admission candidate](ledger-governance-contract.md) applies these choices to the employee/employer witness, proposes minimum logical bindings and admission/feedback behavior, and identifies independent-review blockers. That candidate remains unadopted.

## Change and Work relationship meanings

The following distinguishes required meanings, not four new primitives or selected edge tags. Work remains an accountable unit within one Change; cross-Change dependencies do not reparent it.

| Relationship | Necessary grounds | Inference that must not follow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change to Change | The dependent pursuit's adopted requirement for a specific decision, condition or contribution, with exact source and version bindings. | Producer acceptance or completion makes the consumer ready. |
| Work to Work | The exact output or condition needed by the consuming Work, including its own applicability and acceptance requirements. | Producer exit or completion proves output suitability. |
| Change to Work | The intent and scope the Work serves, applicable requirements, current authority and bounded resource/delegation grounds. | Approval of the pursuit permits every implementation. |
| Work to Change | Established outcomes contributing to identified completion conditions, with partial and unresolved effects preserved. | Finishing the planned task list proves fulfillment of intent. |

Ownership, dependency, ordering and contribution are distinct meanings. Shared-resource contention can exist without a declared dependency. Known missing grounds remain gaps; partial visibility cannot prove independence or complete coverage. The [dependency graph](change-ledger-structure.md#dependency-graph-as-a-view), reverse links, readiness and explanations remain derived Views. A circular chain of assertions cannot supply its own missing evidence.

## Decision and execution boundaries

| Boundary | What must be established | Illustrative feedback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed intent to committed pursuit | Applicable adopted Checks, authorized adoption, scope, anticipated obligations and actual required approval decisions. Substantive feasibility questions are Checks, not a separate engine criterion. | Missing cost estimate or accountable sponsor. |
| Work to execution | The exact action, subject and inputs; current authority, applicable requirements, resource limits and supported native guards/recovery. | Pursuit committed, but the selected provider is not approved for this dataset. |

These are semantic boundaries, not new wire states. Accepting a proposal into history is not committing its intent. Committing a pursuit is not admitting every future Work item, and a passing assessment does not perform either transition. Commitment can itself create obligations; applicable Check requirements and authority must account for that decision boundary rather than defer every safeguard until execution.

A feasibility Check can assess the justified basis for the next bounded action or challenge consequential assumptions with rigor proportionate to consequences. Its exact adopted question and passing condition determine the requirement; this is not a universal engine-owned feasibility veto, certainty of eventual success or proof that every constraint has been discovered. The engine must preserve truthful grounds, enforce applicable Checks and refuse unsupported operations. Missing required evidence blocks the dependent action; it does not become a false assessment, invalidate independent work or make uncertainty disappear.

Bounded investigation may be the next authorized Work precisely because broader feasibility is unresolved. Investigation has its own authority, disclosure, resource and recovery limits; it is not a free exception. Existing delegation can provide those limits without another confirmation ceremony. This does not restore a universal release-wide feasibility questionnaire or require a model call before every operation. Domain questions belong in applicable Checks; truthful grounds, supported semantics and enforcement of those requirements are mandatory engine responsibilities.

At the effect boundary, relevant changed inputs, policy, permissions or resource availability need supported revalidation or conflict handling. Earlier validation is not a permanent execution permit. Guarantees cover the supported host/native bindings and their actual enforcement: unrestricted access outside them can bypass ChangeKernel, and a model's confidence cannot repair that boundary.

## System One judgments and predefined feedback

The approved direction adds focused semantic assessment where code alone cannot interpret meaning, without transferring authority or runtime ownership to a model.

| Responsibility | Intended mechanism and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Known identity/version checks, explicit limits, approval records and native permissions | Deterministic validation and qualified native guards; an agent or model cannot override them with a passing flag. |
| Bounded semantic questions | A supported System One evaluator such as Jev assesses explicit authorized inputs against exact questions and criteria. Examples include whether supplied Work addresses a named prerequisite or a plan describes recovery. |
| Feedback presentation | Code maps qualified results to predefined messages and exact requirement/source references. Multiple issues can coexist; a single selected issue or aggregate score cannot hide another mandatory failure. |
| Commitment and execution | The backend enforces adopted Check requirements using eligible assessments, current authority and native guards. Model judgments do not supply consent, facts outside their evidence or permission to act. |

A qualified semantic evaluator may supply an adopted Check, not only optional advice. Retain consequential assessment identity, exact subject/input/question/model/settings bindings, outcome and required limitations under the [Check contract](checks.md#results-feedback-and-progression). Reuse inherited bindings rather than repeat them. Required source material and detailed results remain referenced. Ordinary live feedback is not automatically a new lifecycle record, but consequential judgment cannot survive only in a disposable presentation.

Distinguish substantive support or contradiction from missing inputs, uncertain judgment, stale grounds, unsupported evaluation and service failure. Predefined answer spaces must not force those cases into apparent success. Distribution concentration is not proof of truth or complete policy coverage; qualification must include confidently wrong judgments, not merely low-confidence cases. Exact thresholds, allowed automation and escalation depend on the adopted question, domain and consequences, not generic example values.

Feedback names the exact assessed action, applicable requirements and policy versions, established grounds, limitations, blockers and possible resolution needs, subject to disclosure. An agent can be told an operation is unavailable without receiving confidential policy details. Identifying a collection need neither authorizes collection nor runs a missing Check. Supported evaluation itself needs authorized disclosure and resource limits; no implicit provider call on every edit, read or query is selected.

Independent questions over the same authorized state may be evaluated together; dependent questions need their actual earlier results or new inputs. Reuse requires exact meaning and relevant dependency/freshness coverage. Evaluate aggregate retrieval, request, token, repair/review, cost and end-to-end latency, rather than treating fast inference as zero cost. No model call or latency experiment has been run for this direction.

## Review witnesses and unresolved selection

These are required paper-review targets, not executed tests or findings:

| Case | Required distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Producer tests package v1; consumer proposes publishing v2 | Producer success cannot satisfy evidence requirements for a different artifact. Current publication authority remains separate. |
| Individually feasible Work items share a budget | Joint resource limits and native concurrency control cannot be inferred from separate passes or absent dependency edges. |
| Policy, relationship or intent changes after assessment | Preserve historical meaning; re-establish affected future admission and handle in-flight responsibility explicitly. |
| Partial completion, circular support or lost reply | Do not invent fulfillment, prerequisite evidence, rollback or safe repetition. |
| Company Work viewed from the employee's Ledger | View selection cannot change governing obligations or disclose unrelated private material. |
| Missing input, partial policy context or confidently wrong semantic assessment | A constrained output is not complete evidence or correctness; qualify the actual question and enforcement boundary. |

Select only what the next consumed slice needs: concrete authentication/representation for the approved person/organization identity model; relationship authority and adoption/applicability of Check requirements; pending-result references and exact material interpretation; relevant-dependency/concurrency validation; bounded feasibility questions; and native acceptance/recovery. Semantic evaluation additionally needs exact supported model/definition/settings, feedback meanings, disclosure, measured error/latency limits and failure handling before it can support admission. Independent review is still required; no concrete contract is adopted here.

The [implementation plan](../../product/plan.md#relationship-policy-and-admission-design-gate) sequences this work. No new scheduler, agent inner loop, generic policy runtime or self-authorizing model is selected.
