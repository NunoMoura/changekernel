# Independent Pursuits and Cooperative Intent

## Cooperation witness and proposal boundaries

**The six interaction refinements are now user-adopted product intent; the earlier ten experience ideas remain proposals.** Neither adoption nor this hypothetical witness selects protocols, a schema or implementation. The question is: what becomes possible when many owner-controlled pursuits interact without surrendering their independence? This page illustrates the [adopted property/value map](change-ledger-properties.md), using the [initial research](../system/research/change-ledger-property-gaps.md) as bounded context rather than proof that these ideas are globally novel. No hypothetical example below has been executed.

The central possibility is **cooperation without assimilation**. Owners can adopt a shared contribution, negotiate compatible objectives, reuse results or separate again while their original pursuits remain identifiable and accountable. A shared undertaking does not require everyone to transfer ownership, reveal a whole Ledger or accept the same definition of success.

The primitives remain Change and Ledger. A contribution, proposal, dependency or agreement below denotes an explicit relationship, accepted decision or Work within those primitives, not a newly selected canonical store, protocol service or universal scheduler. A shared Change still requires a supported governing owner and authority model; it cannot be a file with several competing writers.

## Earlier creative ideas, organized by value

The earlier ten ideas are retained here as proposals. Their names describe experiences, not entities or API signatures.

| Experience idea | Property leverage | Distinct value to explore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intent passport | User-owned continuity and intent portability. | A pursuit moves across supported application surfaces with its meaning intact, without carrying credentials or assumed execution rights. |
| Negotiated delegation | Compositional collaboration and transferable pursuit. | A contributor offers a bounded result and conditions before the owner adopts the contribution. |
| Intent-aware diff | Evolving intent and agent-independent understanding. | Review which objective, constraint, assumption or completion condition changed, not only which artifact bytes changed. |
| Explicit counterproposal | Evolving intent and attributable adoption. | An unavailable goal produces an inspectable tradeoff proposal rather than silent redefinition of success. |
| Intent commons | Demand convergence and secure contextual participation. | Selectively discover opportunities for collective action without publishing private Ledgers or automatically merging intentions. |
| Contribution chains | Contribution reuse and exact acceptance. | A result supports several independent pursuits with recipient-specific applicability and rights. |
| Deliberate alternative worlds | Forkable intent and consolidation. | Compare alternative future strategies and adopt selected contributions without claiming to reverse native effects. |
| Obligation radar | Ledger coordination and responsibility continuity. | Surface unresolved consequences that remain after individual tasks appear finished. |
| Context compiler | Code-use efficiency and agent-independent understanding. | Assemble compact, purpose-specific grounded Views while preserving mandatory constraints, unknowns and coverage. |
| Capability-independent pursuits | Capability decomposition and capability-gap feedback. | The pursuit survives an unavailable or changed application capability; its stored plan does not become current permission. |

## Adopted interaction refinements

These user-adopted refinements complement rather than replace the original properties. Each still needs a concrete positive case and consequential counterexample before any representation is chosen.

The adopted table lives in the [property map](change-ledger-properties.md#adopted-interaction-refinements), with user value, example, necessary distinctions and the disposition of each question. It establishes owner-preserving cooperation, partial convergence, negotiable contribution commitments, dependency-impact visibility, graceful separation and selective compatibility disclosure. Their shared value is cooperation that exposes what was actually accepted without requiring complete convergence or surrender of the original pursuits. The [semantic closure pass](change-ledger-semantic-closure.md) reconciles existing rules while reciprocal-commitment and withdrawal/release defaults still require user adoption. Neither reconciliation nor property adoption establishes implementation or a selected protocol.

No refinement grants automatic native rights, enforceable legal obligations, an atomic multi-owner transaction, guaranteed outcome lookup or revocation of previously disclosed plaintext. The format can preserve the decisions and conditions; a supported host or binding must establish any corresponding enforcement claim.

## Worked imagination: three events, one useful contribution

This hypothetical witness tests the proposed distinctions without selecting an application, executor, wire schema or transfer protocol.

1. **Independent origins.** Ana's Change pursues an accessible music workshop. Ben's Change pursues a low-cost community gathering. Cora's Change pursues a quiet afternoon reading event. Each owner has a personal Ledger and different completion conditions; shared topic and possible venue do not establish one intent.
2. **Selected common ground.** They authorize disclosure of the requirements relevant to one venue assessment. An agent proposes a shared inspection objective covering step-free entry, room capacity and noise constraints. Private negotiations and unrelated Ledger material remain outside the disclosed scope. If needed grounds are missing, compatibility remains limited or unresolved.
3. **An explicit cooperation choice.** The owners can commission an adequate contribution without consolidating their original pursuits. If the inspection itself needs independent adoption, continuation and conclusion, an explicitly governed related Change has one supported owner and its own scope. This example chooses no ownership model or reciprocal acceptance protocol for it.
4. **Negotiation before reliance.** The contributor offers a dated observation with specified coverage, not a universal assurance that every event will succeed. Each owner adopts whether that offer meets the relevant needs of their own Change. No owner silently grants authority over another's resources.
5. **One result, different judgments.** The retained inspection supports Ana's access condition and Ben's capacity condition but finds Cora's noise condition unsatisfied. Recipient decisions remain separate. Producer completion does not make all three pursuits fulfilled, and Cora's refusal does not erase the useful contribution to Ana and Ben.
6. **An alternative and a correction.** Cora may adopt a linked alternative pursuit or revise the same pursuit explicitly. A later corrected capacity observation must preserve the earlier version and expose affected reliance where that relationship is known. Ben re-establishes affected future admission; Ana's unrelated accepted access finding is not automatically invalidated. No global knowledge or automatic effect is assumed.
7. **Separation with consequences.** Ben withdraws from future shared exploration. Any established cost, accepted responsibility, unknown outcome or retained evidence requirement remains discoverable and consequential. Changing a participation label does not discharge it or let another party rewrite Ben's history.

The interesting result is not a single shared task list. It is several independent pursuits that can explain what they share, what they accepted differently and what remains their own responsibility.

## What a fresh agent should be able to explain

Before designing fields or methods, require explanatory answers at a selected, authorized coverage boundary:

- What is common, what differs and which overlap was actually adopted?
- Who owns each pursuit, and who can decide, contribute or continue within the supported scope?
- Which exact result/version does each recipient rely on, under which conditions?
- What did each participant accept, reject or leave undecided?
- What changed, which known future commitments need reassessment and what is not known about undisclosed relationships?
- What survives withdrawal, transfer, stopping or an agent/session change?

A code-use consumer should be able to retrieve bounded selected pursuits and their authorized relationship grounds, compare the relevant versions, and expand exact evidence without reading every participant's transcript. This is a proposed consumption pattern, not a public API. Partial visibility stays partial, reading launches no Work, and the resulting View does not confer current effect permission.

## Promising direction and remaining choices

The adopted direction is **partial convergence with independently accepted contributions**. It combines owner control, practical cooperation and contribution reuse without requiring universal agreement or another top-level primitive. Negotiated commitments and impact visibility make that cooperation understandable; selective disclosure and graceful separation preserve independence.

Next consider whether any further innovation adds distinct user value, distill the adopted properties without losing their distinctions, and challenge this witness with incompatible scope, missing evidence, delayed results and unknown effects. New ideas remain proposals until separately adopted. Only then compare representation options. Independent design review, research expansion and any runtime hypothesis test remain separate steps; no experiment or implementation is authorized by this brainstorming page.
