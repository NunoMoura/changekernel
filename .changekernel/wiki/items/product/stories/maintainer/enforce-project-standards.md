# Preserve Shared Commitments

A Maintainer adopts domain policy in Wiki and selects exact stage-specific Checks or Packs to evaluate it. Wiki owns accepted meaning, scope and exceptions; evaluators supply methods, not a competing policy ledger. A concise adopted reference can identify a machine-defined rule without duplicating its logic. First-party Packs are optional reusable definitions using the same contract as company-authored Checks.

Agents prepare attributable cases and perform authorized work. ChangeKernel owns explicit transversal validation for Decision, Planning, Implementation and Review; adopted domain Checks assess field-specific requirements. Either may use computation or bounded semantic assessment and retain Boolean results, Evidence and corrective feedback. Domain policy cannot disable backend stage validation, and backend reasoning cannot replace a failed required domain assessment. Exact subjects, current authority, isolation, budgets and durable progression remain enforced. Reports identify both owners and their stage-specific coverage without claiming that a pass proves arbitrary truth.

A proposed commitment revision is assessed as a revision. It cannot activate its own exemption or weaker evaluator, but old policy need not be frozen forever: its authorized revision procedure governs the transition. Missing required inputs, unchecked conditions and unavailable execution cannot be reported as validation. Skills may guide work; only actual runtime boundaries prevent forbidden effects before they occur.
