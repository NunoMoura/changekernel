## Manager restart-evidence investigation — decision pending

**Outcome: a concrete service-backed candidate is now identified and its ordering exercised in a disposable logical model. It is not adopted, implemented or qualified.** The local-file ambiguity is not solved by another local marker. The candidate adds an independently protected, durable, linearizable restart witness, with etcd's documented KV transaction/read contract as a specific mechanism to evaluate. A database-backed witness would be an explicit additional control dependency, not a silent reversal of the file-first lifecycle store or the historical SQLite freeze.

The narrow mechanism is **pre-armed unclean state**, not a last-second error notification:

1. From a currently certified closed generation, the exclusively authorized host atomically commits `OPEN` for a fresh exact writer claim **before any A/B custody I/O**, including startup reads, registration and root initialization. Unknown claim outcome permits no local I/O until resolved under that same live claim; a successor cannot steal an orphaned `OPEN` claim.
2. All custody access uses the one observed writer route. A custody I/O error prevents closing. If the observer dies before persisting a poison bit, the already durable `OPEN` remains sufficient to block a successor. A healthy writer killed while open is also blocked; this is clean-restart support, not arbitrary crash recovery.
3. Clean shutdown drains the writer, completes local barriers and all potentially failing custody-handle/cleanup I/O, and seals an exact bounded root image. Only after **no further custody I/O is possible** may an atomic witness comparison publish `CLOSED` for that same claim and image. This control does not replace accepted-prefix checkpoints or promote visible Trace suffixes.
4. A successor uses fresh authenticated witness evidence, rejects changed authority identity/missing controls, arms its own claim, and only then reads/verifies the retained image and current A/B eligibility. Neither a matching local image alone nor an old cursor/certificate authorizes reopening. A mismatched image is blocked, not reconstructed.

**Important result-delivery distinction:** a lost witness RPC reply is not proof the transaction failed. A fresh linearizable read may establish that the exact close was committed; then the ambiguity is resolved by independent current authority, not by re-fsync or a local filename. Conversely, an uncertain/failed local storage barrier must never be followed by a close request. The witness's own storage/health/corruption failures remain subject to its qualified failure model; they cannot be relabeled as harmless transport loss. This clarifies B's “unknown close” wording without waiving its local-I/O incident requirement.

### Concrete candidate and deployment tradeoff

The candidate mapping is a small permanent per-custody-root control entry in an **independently administered etcd quorum**, containing an authority incarnation, non-reused generation/claim and exact seal binding. Use atomic compare-and-update transactions and current linearizable reads. Do not use stale `serializable` reads, watches, expiring leases, elapsed time or deletion as writer/health authority. A delayed close from an old generation must fail its comparison. This does not make an etcd lease capable of fencing a native filesystem writer: actual predecessor exclusion, protected handles and single-host containment remain A/B requirements.

A three-member deployment is a concrete starting candidate for tolerating one member failure under etcd's documented model. Three processes on the same laptop/storage do **not** establish the independent failure domains needed here. Required costs include protected service credentials and network routes, quorum availability, explicit member/storage/corruption policy, operations ownership and exact release qualification. Safe reentry and close would depend on this service being available. Live-operation/delivery behavior during witness loss must be fixed in the composed profile; the model does not certify an offline lease or cached health shortcut.

The witness carries restart-control metadata, not What/Why, a second Change Trace log or source-effect receipts. Its storage is nevertheless authoritative and its metadata disclosure/retention must be authorized. A full witness rollback, same-machine restore, restored quorum or revision bump is not evidence of the missing later history. etcd snapshot restoration creates a new logical cluster; automatic acceptance of that replacement, or of a larger revision alone, is forbidden. Correlation/Byzantine storage failures and loss of all surviving authority are not made safe by saying “three nodes.” No reset, automatic restore or new root/Change ID may hide unknown effects.

A **hardware-backed TPM 2.0 NV guard** is an alternative worth a separate device-specific investigation if a single-machine/offline first profile is required. It is not selected or qualified. The inspected reference distinguishes orderly RAM-backed NV from non-orderly state, delegates final persistence to platform code and enters failure mode on commit failure. Its file-backed simulator uses `fwrite`/`fflush`, not a demonstrated independent durable device. Generic “TPM present,” an NV attribute or a software emulator therefore does not close power-loss, error-recovery, clear/replacement, authentication, endurance or rollback obligations. No TPM device was opened, enrolled, cleared or modified; no hardware capability is assumed for this machine.

**Decision owed by the user:** may the first supported file-first profile require a separate restart-witness service, or must it operate on one machine without that service? The manager recommends the etcd-backed candidate for a bounded next evaluation **if that service dependency is acceptable**, because its documented durable-read/atomic-transaction semantics directly address the gap. Do not make it a mandatory dependency of the reusable engine or quietly impose a cluster on the local-only profile.
