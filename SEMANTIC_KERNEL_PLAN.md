### Required behavior

1. **Pre-I/O admission:** snapshot all five limits as own data fields before any Store call or `await`. Reject missing/accessor/non-number fields, NaN, infinity, fractions, zero, negatives and unsafe integers with typed invalid input and zero Store calls. Later caller mutation cannot change admitted limits. This is passive-data validation, not a hostile-proxy sandbox; no implicit defaults.
2. **Descriptors and budgets:** check returned entry count independently of Store compliance. Count lossless UTF-8 path bytes against remaining budget without first allocating a full encoded copy. Enforce document count before loading blobs. Preserve path spelling, modes/OIDs, duplicates and file/descendant rejection; the unchanged pure corpus remains the final content/descriptor validator. No normalization or case folding.
3. **Inclusive bytes:** at positive remaining budget, request the smaller of per-document cap and remaining total. At zero, request exactly one byte to distinguish an empty blob under the Store's positive-cap API; accept only zero bytes. Nonempty/overflow fails as a typed limit outcome. Recheck returned lengths even if a Store ignores its cap; do not decode/copy over-budget content or return partial success. The one-byte probe does not increase admitted corpus bytes or prove host-allocation containment.
4. **Typed failures:** distinguish invalid input, source selection/binding, Store failures and corpus validation. Retain structured Store code/operation for snapshot/tree/blob failures and corpus code/location. Material reads use a shared detailed source-resolution path; the existing `resolveProjectSource` wrapper keeps its public-facing shape/mapping without resolving twice. No string parsing, issue-catalog/port/wire/public-barrel-export change or private-cause leakage through old responses; internal helper exports between the two scoped query modules are allowed.
5. **Native reads:** split read-path admission from writer portability policy. Admit exact lossless UTF-8 relative paths, including non-NFC, literal glob/pathspec-looking names and valid control-character filename data. Reject absolute/drive, NUL, backslash, empty/dot/traversal segments and malformed Unicode. Do not import writer-only NFC, glob, reserved-name or 4,096-byte limits into reads. Retain literal pathspecs, NUL protocols, exact returned-path matching, finite Store observation bounds and no filesystem dereference. Unsupported host/process limits remain typed failures, not universal filename support.
6. **Order and writer fence:** validate recursive `ls-tree -r -z` in native UTF-8 byte order, with nested/prefix cases checked against Git. Preserve native order; reject malformed/duplicate/out-of-order responses, never sort broken responses into validity. Leave mutation admission/order, private writer-side base-tree handling, digests, authority and CAS unchanged.
7. **Binding and purity:** resolve the selector once; retain repository/object-format, exact commit and blob checks. Keep completeness checks. No closure-walk optimization, batch session, cache, fallback discovery, fetch, working-tree/ignore consultation, link interpretation or new authority inference.
