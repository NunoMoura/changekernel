# Preserve Shared Commitments

A Maintainer can assess a Change against relevant commitments, assumptions and authority without maintaining a separate policy Pack. Semantic feedback distinguishes intent fit, consistency, reachability, preservation, justification and authority in the current stage and scope.

A proposed commitment revision is evaluated as such, not rejected merely for differing from old prose. The candidate cannot use its own waiver, label, discovery rule or relevance ranking to remove binding obligations. More corpus content or a higher aggregate score cannot dilute a binding violation.

Tests, tools, models and people provide bounded support. Supported, contradicted and unresolved findings preserve reasons and limitations. Consequential uncertainty calls for appropriate inquiry or explicit scoped treatment, not an invented pass.
