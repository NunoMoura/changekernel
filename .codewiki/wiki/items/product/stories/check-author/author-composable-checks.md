# Contribute Verification

A contributor adds a useful test, measurement, research result or review procedure to support semantic inquiry. Obligations and reusable procedure knowledge remain in Wiki; ordinary verification methods need no separate Check Pack or Skill enrollment.

Feedback identifies exact subject, tested conditions, evidence, limits and what remains unresolved. A test failure may contradict a particular implementation claim; missing execution is an operational stop, not a refutation of intent. Model confidence alone is not proof.

The contributor cannot weaken applicable obligations through a chosen label or proposed waiver. Revisions to obligations require explicit scoped authority and preserved reasons. Real verification remains available without restoring a parallel policy ontology.
