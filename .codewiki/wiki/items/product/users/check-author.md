# Verification Contributor

A verification contributor improves the evidence and methods used to challenge project knowledge and proposed Changes. They may contribute tests, measurements, research, review procedures or tools for any team domain.

Applicable obligations and procedural knowledge belong in Wiki. Contributors do not need to author or adopt a separate CodeWiki Check Pack, duplicate a rubric in a Skill, or choose a classification that controls their own approval.

A useful method identifies exact subject, conditions, support and limits. Passing a test does not prove every semantic claim; unavailable execution and unsupported conclusions remain explicit. Independent evidence and critical review can challenge the proposal without becoming unscoped acceptance or execution authority.
