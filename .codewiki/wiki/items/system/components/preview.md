# Preview

Preview starts bounded local targets and captures browser or interaction observations for one exact Gate subject commit/tree. It returns provenance-bound Evidence metadata. Screenshots, recordings, pages, logs, and temporary processes remain outside canonical semantic authority and are disposable unless retention policy admits exact material.

Preview cannot pass a Check, alter its subject, publish a product, mutate provider state, or reuse observations across changed subject, configuration, route, or environment identity. Unavailable Preview produces explicit operational stop or missing Evidence, never a fabricated semantic failure.
