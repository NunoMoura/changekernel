# Decision

Decision evaluates whether a proposed Change is worth pursuing under its expressed intent, scope, tradeoffs, and authority. It accepts a justified transition hypothesis with credible feasibility, not a complete implementation plan or universal reachability proof.

Relevant knowledge includes applicable commitments, competing purposes, assumptions, alternatives, uncertainty, and prior reasons for rejection. Contradiction with an existing rule may be an intentional authorized revision; a candidate's proposed waiver is not its own permission. Feedback uses the shared [semantic validation](checks.md) distinctions rather than a mandatory stage-specific Check Pack.

The outcome and its grounds remain attributable to the exact proposal and baseline. Acceptance to proceed is distinct from adoption of a particular Wiki consequence, realization, merge, publication, or deployment. Deferred, rejected, withdrawn, and superseded proposals retain the conditions and reasons that explain their disposition.

Intent can evolve through inquiry. A material revision must be explicit and revisit Decision, preserving the original proposal and the reason for change. Missing consequential context is reported, not treated as irrelevance or silently repaired by a model. See [Decision to Planning](../flows/decision-to-planning.md).
