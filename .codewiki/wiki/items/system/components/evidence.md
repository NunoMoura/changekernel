# Evidence

Evidence, source material, Wiki meaning, and judgment are separate. An Evidence Record owns compact provenance-bound observation metadata over exact material. A Check Result separately judges one exact Gate subject and declared Evidence. Evidence never becomes accepted meaning, lifecycle authority, or a second copy of source payload merely because a tool, model, provider, or human produced it.

An Evidence Record binds subject, observation schema/digest, material refs, producer, method, tool/configuration, custody receipt, observation time, freshness, coverage, sensitivity, limitations, and authority level. Authority may be `asserted`, `observed`, `verified`, or `approved`; sharing or importing a record transfers neither applicability nor acceptance.

Raw Data or a native owner keeps exact bytes and immutable protocol records outside Wiki. Evidence cites sorted exact refs to Raw Data revisions/slices, Git artifacts, DSH or Plugin/provider receipts, Change operations, Actor attestations, or migration receipts. A bounded observation body may contain measurements, interpreted facts, stance, and limitations, but not opaque execution state, hidden reasoning, credentials, or copied source payload without policy.

Web pages, PDFs, provider responses, search results, and deterministic extractions enter policy-bound Raw Data before Evidence cites them. Model synthesis alone is not source Evidence. Human approvals and authenticated native provider facts may cite their exact operations/receipts without redundant payload copies. Bounded importers for SARIF, JUnit, coverage, SBOM, Pact, OpenAPI, and provider receipts follow the same split.

Capture policy chooses `metadata_only | cited_slices | full_revision`; retention chooses `transient | ttl | while_referenced | pinned` within operator/legal constraints. Missing bytes cannot be reconstructed as the same revision. Refetch creates another revision and Evidence. Records remain immutable when material changes, expires, is deleted, or becomes inaccessible; current support/freshness derives from new observations.

Changed, missing, revoked, expired, or inaccessible material stales dependent support and affected Result reuse. Policy may open Change Intake, but observations never rewrite Wiki, Trace, Results, or Git refs. DSH internal Session bytes remain private execution evidence and never substitute for accepted context, feedback, or lifecycle facts.
