# Active Change compatibility

## Requirement

The exact Candidate must have complete accepted active Changes coverage and no unresolved semantic contradiction, duplicate Change, or improper supersession.

## Pass

Pass only when the supplied Decision subject proves complete active-Change coverage and compatible meaning.

## Fail

Fail when coverage is missing, stale, contradictory, duplicative, or improperly supersedes accepted meaning.

## Feedback

Identify exact accepted Change and conflicting or missing semantic relationship. Do not author replacement meaning.
