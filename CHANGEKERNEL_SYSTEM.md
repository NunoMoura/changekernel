# ChangeKernel System

ChangeKernel preserves owner-controlled pursuits' intent, decisions, evidence and accountable progress across sessions, agents and authorized collaborators—not prescribed process or a replacement for judgment.

This is desired state, not proof of implemented capability. Explicit proposals remain unadopted; design approval grants no implementation permission. [Status](CHANGEKERNEL_STATUS.md) owns execution/gaps until qualified Change operations can; [AGENTS](AGENTS.md) owns procedure and document limits; [README](README.md) is the entrypoint. No parallel specification or fabricated governance. Historical meaning survives design revision.

## Product and boundaries

### Change and Ledger

A **Change** holds an owner-controlled pursuit's developing intent contract and durable history, from one action to months—not an action, prompt, task list or conversation. Drafting commits nothing; agreement binds exact terms without fully planning pursuit. Retain alterations, learning, grounds, failures/disproved hypotheses, abandonment and unresolved outcomes. Goals belong within intent.

A **Ledger** is its owner's stable logical collection, not an engine directory: each person has one across apps, sessions, devices, employer roles and collectives. Agents represent identified owners; sessions create none. No silent default selection. Authorized Ledger inclusion establishes a Change's single personal or collective owner, not its location. A collective has its own stable owner/Ledger independent of membership, not a union/writable replica of personal Ledgers. Identity, representation, inclusion and addressing mechanisms remain open.

**Ledger and Change are the only core primitives.** Standalone pursuits need no peer, collective, app, repository or native resource. Services are deployment mechanisms; references/participation/versions add no universal State store. Relations/work units live in Change histories; graphs/indexes/queues are derived. No prescribed roles/phases/tactics or undeclared semantic veto. Human/software participants share accountability principles; adopted requirements govern assessment, while adaptation within discretion is normal.

### Intent contract: WHY, WHAT, HOW

These are dimensions, not primitives, stores, stages, fixed plans or required fields. Operations develop them without one-to-one mapping; Views project them.

| Dimension | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| WHY | Problem/opportunity, priorities, benefit/tradeoffs, sources and uncertainty. Preserve stated reasons, never invent them. User-endorsed values lead; observation alone cannot establish them. |
| WHAT | Desired conditions/outcomes, scope/exclusions, constraints versus discretion, criteria and evidence—not artifact inventory. Artifact changes bind only as adopted requirements; several outcomes may be acceptable. |
| HOW | Provisional approach, hypotheses and grounds for adaptation within authority/limits. Only explicit methodological constraints bind; tactics within discretion need no readoption. |

Establish intent, constraints, progress/fulfillment criteria and unknowns. Candidate edits are not requirements or proof of agreement/improvement; resource scope grants no native permission. Bound unknowns at affected decisions, not by demanding blueprints or blocking unrelated work. Omissions grant no unlimited discretion.

Before undertaking, retain a feasibility/capability/resource/dependency/authority/commitment assessment with grounds, limitations and coverage. Missing history proves no absence of conflict; new commitments cannot silently override old ones. Revalidate relevant changed grounds without exhaustive impact detection or whole-Ledger invalidation. Owner adoption and agent undertaking bind identical explicit terms, not provisional context. Assessment is not consent or an unrestricted veto over user values.

Pursue fulfillment through inquiry, blockers, adaptation or revision—not response completion. Session loss erases no commitment; unfinished intent grants no expanded scope, indefinite/background work or exemption from fulfillment evidence/acceptance.

### Understanding, clarification and judgment

Preserve decision-relevant understanding and its sources, alternatives, assumptions, support/challenge/unknown assessments and limitations—not every private thought, a compulsory argument graph or final complete understanding. Owners determine what matters/adopt criteria; agents may assess, challenge assumptions, identify the wrong problem and propose alternatives. Shared obligations need applicable parties' decisions. Recording must not misstate commitments, but ChangeKernel is no universal moral, psychological, communication or privacy arbiter.

Distinguish expressed intent, interpretation, proposed terms and commitment. Acceptance or a coherent explanation proves neither truth nor endorsement. Motivating, explanatory and justificatory reasons can differ; attributable grounds need not reveal infallible motives. Equal conclusions with different grounds may need different reconsideration. Inference establishes no other's decision/permission; retrospective explanation supplies no contemporary approval/grounds. Evidence correction, intent revision and execution repair differ.

Clarification asks **what is meant**; revision asks **what becomes binding**. A limit may always have been intended but become explicit now; revision need not assert changed intent, and correcting interpretation need not revise agreement.

| Change | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Incomplete draft clarified | Elaborate; no binding terms yet |
| Interpretation/fact/explanation corrected | Attributed Contribute with grounds |
| Approach adapted within discretion | Contribute; no owner readoption |
| Supporting grounds/circumstances changed | Reassess/revalidate affected decisions where required |
| Adopted requirements, criteria, scope or methods changed | Applicable Decide revision |
| Permissions/disclosure changed | Applicable authority Decide, not necessarily intent revision |

Prose or a “clarification/no change” label cannot override terms. Unresolved binding effect/permission requires exact current terms, sources, proposed meaning/effect and applicable participants' confirmation before reliance, with attributable explanations/confirmations retained. Changed terms/permissions still need their decisions; clear corrections/adaptations do not. Only affected actions wait. Kernel checks subjects, references and authority, not perfect semantic equivalence; interface/authentication remain open, with no semantic oracle selected.

Contested attribution/assessment stays attributable without rewritten decisions. Investigating removal is not removal approval; failed experiments are not failed Changes. Challenges alone create no veto; authority/integrity failures or changed dependent grounds still trigger guards. Successor intent cannot replace authorized revision. Unclear meaning, uncertain approach, unknown effects and unverifiable permission need different responses—not one confidence score. Hypotheses support inquiry without proving truth/permission; no compulsory experiment cycle. Tests confer no consent/guaranteed success; metrics alone prove no improvement. Learning spends within agreed bounds.

### Progress, completion and readiness

Understanding progress is evidenced improvement in decision-relevant understanding of values, conditions or viable approaches—not confidence; discovering unknown uncertainty may qualify. Outcome progress is evidenced improvement against agreed intent/criteria—not activity or changed targets. Activity/failure may advance either, both or neither. Learning outcomes still need fulfillment evidence/acceptance. Changed reality, meaning, understanding and commitment do not automatically prove one another, authority or convergence.

Completion may combine native values, geography/time or owner judgment. Separate completion, optimization and execution limits; specify sources/windows, deadlines and reach-once versus maintenance. Missing evidence is unknown; edits/tasks/proxies prove neither native outcomes nor causation. Flat all-criteria fulfillment is not the general model. Composition, applicability, temporal evaluation and preauthorized closure remain open; define nonvacuous fulfillment before replacing A's settlement safeguards.

### Ownership and cooperation

Receiving/copying/hosting/sharing/working transfers no ownership. Collective initiation differs from its actor; later adoption cannot retroactively own a personal Change. Forks retain distinct pursuits, initiating owners and lineage, not inherited permissions/commitments. Sharing, delivery, delegation, handoff, consolidation and material/responsibility transfer differ.

Recipients decide on exact disclosed contributions without a second writable source continuation. Copies/restoration/import confer no source authority, recipient adoption or native replay. Multi-party agreements need applicable parties' exact-term decisions; leaving erases no responsibility. Personal participation differs from collective representation; external contributors need not join. Membership alone authorizes neither representation, decisions, receipt nor action. Voting, administrator, delegation and collective-exit policies remain open; owner withdrawal remains available.

### Resource-scoped participation and native State

Inter-Ledger communication is bounded by native resource scopes and explicit participation terms. A native resource is interpreted by its app, with identity/authority context not guaranteed by names/paths/URLs. Scope names a resource set, namespace or creation boundary and subject/version conditions; it may precede creation without authorizing it. Participation binds identified owners/Ledgers, selected Change interactions, scope, allowed actions, disclosure and authority; association is not admission. These are adopted meanings, not wire/authentication protocols.

Native apps interpret scopes/enforce native permissions; ChangeKernel enforces participation, acceptance and disclosure. Neither grants the other's authority. Apps own State, artifacts, concurrency and schemas; resources remain references, not universal CRUD, a mirror or competing artifact store. App use, membership, relevance or shared agents prove no representation/consent. Bind actor/represented owner; never pool disclosure or imply copying private Ledgers. Expansion, replacement or changed grounds needs applicable authorization/revalidation; renaming proves no continuity. Unverifiable identity/scope/grounds requires limitation. Missing peers block no unrelated pursuit, while dependent decisions/fulfillment still need evidence. **Open question:** purely informational inter-Ledger exchange without a native resource; existing scoping remains until amendment.

References name integration/environment, resource and observed conditions/version; formats/isolation remain open. Fresh reads cannot protect later writes. Retain relevant comparison/decision grounds, attributed observations and receipts beyond temporary context, not universal inventories. Reports are not independent evidence; effects prove no improvement. Internal custody/lifecycle/recovery state remains necessary.

App/team/personal versions select exact contributions/results/native subjects, not moving tips. Association, pending work, builds/deployments or app use imply no Ledger acceptance or collective membership. Changes may span releases; upgrades need exact decisions/native evidence. Application contracts—not ownership alone—govern inclusion, compatibility, support/retention, qualification, recovery, licensing, compensation and maintenance. Audience selection is not isolation; rollback may leave effects. No full-stack framework, package manager, sandbox or marketplace is proposed.

## Authoritative history and material

Retain intent/approval source text inline or as exact Change-owned material without duplication. External references require current native permissions, exact versions/source identities and retained required grounds; disclose unavailability, change and access limits. Hashes, URLs, temporary context, claimed memory and private session links prove neither retention nor continuity. Change disclosure grants no native access.

### Acceptance invariants

| Invariant | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Stable scope / explicit adoption | Scope remains until authorized revision; recording proposals/recommendations/assessments adopts nothing |
| Exact subject / concurrency | Bind action, inputs, intent version, requirements and supporting material; revalidate expected subjects and changed grounds |
| Current authority | Validate at acceptance/disclosure; caller assertions such as `approved: true` are not authority |
| Append-only meaning | Corrections, revisions and supersession are new attributable operations, never rewritten history |
| Original-result recovery | Exact retry identity/inputs resolve original acceptance; reject conflicting reuse |
| Honest uncertainty | Timeout, lost reply, cancellation or disconnect proves neither nonacceptance nor rollback |
| Truthful outcomes | Observation, assessment, producer completion, recipient acceptance and fulfillment differ |
| Withdrawal | Stops future pursuit despite unresolved actions, not pending accountability; no dispatched-action cancellation, rollback or fulfillment follows. Recovery needs current authority. |
| Historical interpretation | Documentation/codec changes cannot retroactively change retained meaning or authority |

Kernel guarantees and user requirements share evidence/evaluation semantics, never authority. Extensions cannot redefine operations, waive guarantees or grant permission; advisory packs activate nothing. Separate definition, adoption, applicability, evaluation and assessment. Start with bounded structural/reference/range/decision/review requirements, not scripts. Bind evidence to exact subject/requirement versions, reviewer/profile, inputs, judgment/uncertainty and applicability grounds. Missing, stale, unsupported or inconclusive required evidence cannot pass. Evaluators need separate authority/budgets outside kernel/custody; prohibit circular/unbounded evaluation and reinterpretation through reruns. Schemas/lifecycle/qualification remain open; no model dependency.

Human approval must not hold a global admission/transaction lock. Prepare exact subjects and revalidate at acceptance; reservation protocols remain open.

## Deterministic Change Views for agent context

**Authorized current/historical Views serve reads; exact-subject operations serve writes.** Private files are not client APIs; export/backup needs separate authority. Project accepted intent/reasons, commitments, constraints, decisions, sourced observations, attributed interpretations/hypotheses, attempts/adaptations, learning, both progress dimensions, blockers, unresolved outcomes and pending work/questions. Keep exact references, versions, failures and contemporary versus retrospective explanations. Summaries cannot replace sources; claims remain claims and proposals are not schedules.

- **Content:** identical qualified source cuts, permitted scope and projection/interpretation definitions yield identical projected content. Current applicability comes from explicit statuses/relationships, not old prose. Qualify head/coverage observations separately for that scope/definition.
- **Coverage:** distinguish processed cutoffs from verified observed heads, not last matching rows. Unknown eligibility/head/lag stays unknown. Disclose exclusions, omissions and missing/unavailable/stale/unsupported grounds only within current permission. Mixed-time cuts imply no global snapshot or clock-derived causality; partial history is not a complete drain/integrity audit.
- **Recovery:** logical cursors address accepted positions, not physical files/offsets. Bind progress to source/scope/definition; recover missed notifications from retained histories. Recover derived updates/progress together, deduplicate, and distinguish accounted filtering from unexplained gaps; never advance completeness through those gaps. Scope/filter changes require renewed coverage accounting. Publish a valid generation or explicit partial coverage, not a half-rebuilt complete result.
- **Boundaries:** rebuilding interprets history without native effects. Notification/projection/rebuild failures veto no unrelated acceptance. Eventual readiness indexes replace no admission checks; inferred suggestions are not adopted relations or a global authoritative relationship graph. No bus, per-Change poller or session transport is required.

Assess uncertainty/tradeoffs/mismatches against exact intent/observations, not a universal completion percentage. Apply current disclosure even to historical projections. Delivery proves no understanding, authority, retention, recovery or native freshness; View wire/cursor/publication and Pi Durable delivery/refresh mechanisms remain open.

## Change operations

Adopted semantic families, not APIs, phases, interchangeable payloads or implementation permission. Interfaces may combine operations, never their effects/preconditions; Clarify, Learn and Explain need no extra families.

### Adopted operation families

| Family | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Authorized initiation opens/includes a draft, not agreement/pursuit permission |
| Contribute | Attributed proposals, questions, interpretations, plans, observations, hypotheses, findings, corrections, explanations, reviews, relations, adaptations and assessments; retain sources, limitations, benefits/harms and uncertainty, adopting nothing |
| Decide | Exact-subject adoption, undertaking, rejection, revision, retraction, reaffirmation, authority or settlement under the applicable rule; no substitute for required term/grant changes |
| Admit action | Record admission/denial against current intent, authority, grounds and native conditions before dispatch, not execution/success |
| Report outcome | Source results/failures/uncertainty and original-action reconciliation, not replay/reopening |

Investigation is authorized host action, not a pre-commit phase. Contributions are neither private reasoning, consent nor host outcomes. Managed View queries require host recording without mutating accepted history.

### Related Changes

Separate pursuits for useful independent agreement, ownership, authority, acceptance or lifecycle—not per task/entity/experiment. Prefer reuse, with no mandatory tree. Relations retain type, exact endpoints/subjects, grounds and uncertainty in attributable histories. A's statement about B creates neither B's decision nor delivery obligation. Dependencies name versioned conditions/outputs, not necessarily whole-Change completion. Allow feedback but not impossible strict-prerequisite cycles; expose partial coverage, not global acyclicity/optimal decomposition.

| Interaction | Surface and distinct effect |
| --- | --- |
| Discover/compare | Authorized Views; similarity establishes no shared ownership/combination |
| Start/fork/split/consolidate/replace | Start + Contribute + applicable Decide; retain lineage, disclosed subjects and separate ownership/histories/responsibilities |
| Context/lineage; reuse grounds | Contribute relevance without obligation; Views + Contribute retain exact authorized grounds/limitations, not recipient adoption/source authority |
| Support/challenge/expected contribution | Contribute findings about particular claims/assumptions or hypothesized benefit, not whole-Change linkage, proven truth/causation/success or veto |
| Propose/adopt dependency | Contribute + Decide; “requires” conditions an identified action, while “completion depends on” needs fulfillment-requirement adoption; name versions and distinguish proposals |
| Changed grounds/resource conflicts | Contribute impact/tradeoffs; applicable Decide revalidates/reaffirms/revises or allocates/prioritizes, without automatic precedence or rewritten history |
| Execute once, assess several consequences | One initiating Admit/Report history; separate evidenced assessments in affected Changes, no pooled grants |
| End relationship/participation | Contribute retracts claims; applicable Decide alters commitments, retaining other Changes/responsibilities |

Discovery/linking/inclusion grants no authority or budget exemption; restructuring erases no effects. Missing grounds can leave assessments inconclusive. Supporting completion proves no other native goal; abandoned tactics need not defeat it. Exact references/lifecycle, concurrent dependency/cycle validation and recovery remain open.

### Work units and execution

Optional bounded work units organize execution within a Change, not independently owned intent. Attempts are worker/run actions: multiple failures need not abandon work, and unit completion is not fulfillment. Briefs retain intent, requested result/investigation, inputs, capabilities, dependencies, authority, limits and evidence expectations—not exhaustive steps or mandatory phases.

Contribute preparation/revision; Views expose derived eligibility; authorized Decide/host coordination allocates/prioritizes/assigns. Requests/queues are not deployment/admission. Claim/dispatch binds unit/attempt/worker, prevents accidental duplicates and revalidates each managed action through Admit. Report sourced outcomes separately from quality, unit acceptance and fulfillment. Cancel/pause/reassign/retry needs current authority/grounds and applicable decisions, never rollback inference or blind replay.

ChangeKernel retains associations, requests, decisions, limits/outcomes; Pi Durable executes. Capacity waits and priority authorize no dispatch/cancellation. Budgets cannot multiply across Changes/units/attempts; coordination prevents guaranteed maximum efficiency. Expiry/loss/cancellation proves no absence of effects; session end closes neither unit nor Change. Retain original-attempt accountability and revalidate queued dispatch. Identity/claims, lifecycle/accounting, partial results and recovery remain open; no capacity automation/universal active-Change quota. Bound active work, not history, learning or responsibility.

## Adopted bounded agreement profile

**A — agreement/authority** covers one individual owner and one identified undertaking agent, not collective/delegated owner decisions or a universal cross-Change reference model. **H** supplies authentic caller/approval binding, native scope and dispatch; protocols remain open. **D** supplies durable identity/retention below.

- **Subjects:** proposals bind owner/Ledger/Change, expected agreement or absence, exact terms, context and unknowns. Decisions name complete immutable subjects, actor/represented owner, authority and dependency references/versions. Equal text/moving tips are not identity. Undertaking names a retained feasibility/commitment assessment, limitations and coverage.
- **Agreement:** either decision order; the second matching decision activates only while both remain effective, the predecessor current and the Change nonterminal. Unmatched decisions may retract. Revision follows the same rule; proposals neither replace nor suspend terms. Preserve supersession and reject stale/mismatched activation.
- **Changed grounds:** stale only explicitly dependent decisions; activation/admission awaits affected-party reaffirmation. Agents cannot renew owner decisions; changed terms need matching new decisions. Restore no superseded predecessor.
- **Authority:** owner control governs adoption/revision, grants, fulfillment and withdrawal; agents undertake for their identified role. Grants target named drafts/bounded draft creation for recording/disclosure, exact agreements for pursuit, or original actions for reconciliation. Draft grants permit no model/tool/native dispatch, investigation or external disclosure; they cannot bootstrap pre-agreement activity.
- **Grants:** named recipient, target, actions, disclosure/native scope and limits; omissions grant nothing. Generation starts at one; replacement/revocation requires and advances the expected generation. Revoked IDs cannot be reused. Each operation needs one complete grant, without union/revision inheritance; new grants cannot revive queued old generations.
- **Dispatch:** current agreement/grant generation, scope, retained grounds and supported native conditions. Revision/revocation/settlement invalidates queued permissions; H revalidates at dispatch. Dispatched actions retain original agreement/grant associations.
- **Readiness/progress:** drafts may lack terms/criteria. Agreement decisions require nonempty binding terms, not criteria/exhaustive answers; undertaking requires the assessment. Progress names current agreement, affected terms, optional applicable criteria, evidence and both progress dimensions—not fulfillment. Adaptation names agreement, grounds and approach.
- **Settlement:** fulfillment requires current agreement, criteria/evidence and resolved relevant blockers; withdrawal needs neither success nor resolved actions. Both stop pursuit, late activation/revision, queued permissions and reopening. Separately authorized accountability retains original-action association/uncertainty without replay.

### Adopted extension to A: agreement references and roles

- **Membership:** material belongs to a named accepted operation in the same owner/Ledger/Change, with exact membership retained at acceptance. Reject spliced, missing, foreign-operation, moving or rebound bindings. Only the current operation's own bindings may be provisional, validated atomically. Attribution/access/retention remain required; references confer no authorship, storage or permission.
- **Selections:** finite explicit binding/context selections, criteria and questions—not dimension slots/tags. Selected terms/criteria bind. Binding terms/criterion definitions cannot also be context, including another reference to the same material. Mixed prose needs an explicit retained proposed-term statement linked to its source, not silent extraction. Decisions bind complete immutable proposal/roles; context cannot weaken artifact/method requirements or imply inherited selections. Context may still interpret terms under clarification rules.
- **Criterion identity:** freeze definition and nonempty exact links to binding terms; changes require fresh identity. Reuse transfers neither terms nor fulfillment; evidence/acceptance names current agreement and criterion.
- **Evolution:** contributions/questions may link affected subjects without dimension tags. Proposal edits require fresh identities; agreed terms/criteria/roles require matching revision decisions. Context links alone resolve no question or replace terms. Durable schema remains open.

### Adopted extension to A: future-material recording

Recording grants may cover fresh contributions/sources within named Changes/limits without per-text renewal; recording grants no investigation, native access or disclosure and establishes no truth, endorsement or requirements.

- **Creation:** one current grant binds recipient, owner/Ledger/Change, A target (draft, agreement or original actions), internal operations and limits. Cover the whole operation and fresh same-record bindings; no prior ingestion, scope union, Ledger wildcard or separate material API. Draft restrictions remain.
- **Acceptance:** retain immutable bytes, recorder/represented owner, attribution and origin: grant ID/generation, target and creating operation/material binding. Origin proves neither authorship nor originality. Record, bindings and charges accept together or none; provisional origins grant nothing. Exact retries recover results without allocation/recharge under current disclosure.
- **Reuse:** grants may select their generation/target's accepted material, including future acceptances; other inputs need explicit scope. Labels, paths/equal bytes establish no origin. Replacement/new grants may select exact IDs/historical origins, without generation/revision inheritance. Origin survives revocation; consuming authority remains current. No cascading revocation of independent grants or revived queues.
- **Disclosure/sources:** creation/use grants no return of content/references/metadata, sharing/export, native fetch or model/tool calls. Separate current disclosure may select accepted origins; source access/retention remains required. H authenticates caller/source; prose is not observation/consent/success. Denied disclosure implies neither rollback nor replay permission.
- **Limits:** bound operations. Cumulative per-grant quotas remain a separate policy choice. If selected, per-operation/total item and payload-byte caps charge each new identity's logical payload, including equal bytes, not references/retries. Usage survives generations; replacement changes caps, not usage. Revalidate scope/generation, applicable limits and source/disclosure at acceptance. Only accepted creation incurs charges; exhaustion blocks creation, not retention. Refused lookup proves no earlier noncommit.
- **Lifecycle:** revocation blocks its grant, not retention/independent accountability. Settlement stops pursuit recording; reconciliation needs current original-action authority. Origin selection cannot migrate authority or reopen pursuit. H/D enforcement, durability and recovery remain unqualified; prove same-record creation, authorized return/reuse under one initial grant, and authority/limit/retry failures.

## Durable identity and storage

### Adopted storage architecture

One owner-local SQLite database per Change holds its originals/results, required material/origins/grounds and acceptance controls in one transaction domain. The Ledger remains a logical collection; membership/custody evidence is authoritative, not a derived View. Ordinary acceptance requires no other Change or View commit; actual dependencies, commitments and native conditions still apply. Exports/copies are not writable originals.

Pi/native state, credentials and current authority remain outside; no Pi/native atomicity, copied writer rights or whole-state rollback protection. Authority layout, client stores, projections and transport are separate choices. Authorized clients may keep writable provisional drafts/context; local save/delivery is not owner acceptance or another writable authoritative continuation. No mandatory paid database service/subscription. Adopted targets: **Rust 1.99.1, edition 2021; SQLite 3.54.1**. Approval/qualification remain separate from implementation.

### Adopted independent-Change profile

- **Entity identity:** qualify Operation, Proposal, Agreement, Assessment, Decision, Grant, Action and Criterion by owner/Ledger/Change; check creation collisions within that namespace. Change IDs are unique within their owner-bound Ledger. Owner, Ledger, Actor, Ground, GroundVersion, Scope, Limit, NativeAction and ContextVersion retain issuing scopes; Action is not NativeAction. Qualify nested subjects, predecessors, equality and persisted request/retry targets, not just display labels. Raw values, filenames and routing defaults establish no qualified identity.
- **Inclusion:** retain authoritative membership/original-custody evidence without an every-acceptance membership update. Missing members are not empty; scans of surviving files cannot prove a complete Ledger. Resolve uncertain creation/inclusion under the original request before writable activation, never a fresh identity. A known original may proceed on its own valid evidence; unresolved membership/custody blocks the affected Change, not automatically the collection.
- **Recovery:** distinguish lost discovery, incomplete collection and invalid original. Validate/fence the affected original without skipping damage or promoting copies. Collection backups declare membership coverage and individual cuts, not unsupported global consistency. Version namespace/interpretation distinctions; never reinterpret old records/retries or presume old stores writable under new rules. No migration/import/takeover. Origin-evidence admissibility, concrete wire mapping, inclusion reconciliation and View protocols remain mechanism gates.

### Adopted trusted-port durable records

Preserve A, unaffected accepted behavior and historical meaning. Rules/IDs/records stay OS-independent; Linux is first, with macOS/Windows requiring later adapter/build/storage tests. This bounded D slice excludes host authentication/dispatch, app access, multi-agent execution, general Views and retrieval.

- **IDs/intake:** distinct typed nonzero 16-byte values persist across restarts/retries/restoration, without authority/native identity. Generate raw candidates through OS-secure adapter randomness; fail on entropy errors, bound zero resampling, with no clock/counter fallback or allocator service. Before submission, durably retain the complete immutable typed request/creation IDs and qualified target; never silently regenerate submitted IDs. Reuse established actor/owner/Ledger identities; atomically distinguish creation collisions from references/retries. Caller retention is trusted-port work; H must qualify Pi persistence reuse, not another journal.
- **Build:** pin `rusqlite` 0.41.0, sys 0.39.0, `prost` 0.14.4; review locks/licenses. Disable rusqlite defaults; enable only `backup`/`limits`. Statically link externally built, checksum-verified official SQLite 3.54.1, not bundled 3.54.0. Require explicit include/library paths, thread safety, no extension loading or double-quoted string literals; disable pkg-config, ambient fallback and Cargo-time downloads. Verify runtime version/source ID/options. Checked-in prost definitions, no protoc. Native/toolchain preparation needs authorization.
- **Encoding:** length-framed Protobuf retains exact original bytes and codec/schema/semantic versions, not Rust-only encoding or required gRPC. Before allocation/decode: 16 MiB record, 1 MiB per material, 8 MiB/64 fresh materials per operation, 4,096 entries per repeated field, depth 32. Defensive ceilings are not cumulative quotas; tighter grants apply. Reject malformed frames, unknown fields/enums/versions, duplicate singular/oneof fields, missing meaningful presence, invalid IDs and duplicate set members; preserve list order. Never reuse retired tags, default meaning or ignore unsupported constraints. Serialization is not canonical; re-encoding/hashes define neither historical identity nor authority/exactly-once effects. Single-current-profile validation follows the qualified representation contract below; review concrete field/tag changes before implementing them.
- **Restoration:** retain immutable request/result, identity/predecessor, historical checks, grant use/snapshot and required material/origins/grounds. References resolve retained earlier records. Reconstruct ordered state/retries under supported historical interpretation, checking linkage/results/bindings/origins/controls—not fresh execute, approvals, IDs, charges or effects. Missing/damaged grounds or unsupported interpretation blocks that original's writable opening; preserve its database without skipping/repair. Refuse unsupported originals before mode mutation.
- **Acceptance:** local WAL with verified `synchronous=FULL`, foreign keys/constraints. Separate creation/opening and bind stored owner/Ledger/Change. One short transaction revalidates prefix, trusted context and grants/limits/sources, inserts full record, material/origins, result and controls, and holds relevant local context guards through commit. No native/host atomicity or model/tool/human waits inside transactions; publish memory/success only after known acceptance.
- **Retry/uncertainty:** compare complete original typed inputs/actor/owner, not serialization/hashes. Exact retries return original results under current source/disclosure without allocation/execution/recharge; conflicting reuse fails. Distinguish accepted, established nonacceptance and indeterminate; denied lookup discloses nothing and proves no noncommit. Commit/I/O errors or lost replies prove no rollback. Fence further writes on the uncertain handle; reopen/validate the same original and resolve its ID. Only established noncommit permits unchanged resubmission, never new IDs/native replay.
- **Custody/maintenance:** trusted local storage, private platform-specific files and an exclusive lifetime writer lock distinct from SQL locks. Refuse competing writers, wrong identity, detectable replacement/corruption or unsupported profile/filesystem; claim no hostile same-account or coherent whole-state rollback protection. Use short internal snapshots, immediate busy refusal only for established noncommit, and bounded passive checkpoints. Checkpointing close needs current maintenance authority; denial/fencing must still permit safe noncheckpointing release. Maintenance failure cannot undo acceptance. Qualify identity/locking/durability/permissions/lifecycle; runtime/temporary storage is unsuitable.
- **Backup/upgrades:** separately authorized quiesced SQLite backup to private destinations; verify originals/completeness and sync snapshot/destination directory before success. Copies support read-only reconstruction, not writes/replay. Refuse live replacement, writable activation, merge/import, migration/downgrade and unsupported versions; never substitute an empty Change or invent complete collection coverage. Normal original-store restart remains required.

### Adopted qualified representation and current development format

These contracts make the independent-Change namespace/intake rules concrete; they select no inclusion catalog, View publication protocol, transport or H authentication mechanism. Qualifiers and intake declarations supply neither origin evidence nor permission. Adoption grants no implementation permission.

- **Keys and membership:** `ChangeKey = (OwnerId, LedgerId, ChangeId)` and `MaterialKey = (establishing ChangeKey, MaterialId)`. Existing bound containers may qualify local entity leaves/predecessors; retain full Identity/participant/authority checks independently. Use complete MaterialKeys in maps, grants/disclosure, origin selections, claims/retained sets, references, role and retry equality. `(B,x)` and `(C,x)` differ; two local references to the same key cannot evade role exclusivity. A retains authorized foreign grounds through its own accepted operation. Its binding is `(IdentityA, local OperationId, MaterialKey)`, resolved against that exact local membership—not latest presence or a foreign operation pointer. Keep foreign establishing-operation evidence separately; it never substitutes for A's membership.
- **Intake:** use explicit fresh, first-retention and known-reuse cases, validated by the trusted boundary rather than an agent flag. Fresh material and pre-existing input without a known binding establish only in the accepting Change; first retention records its kind/provenance/uncertainty, not creation/authorship. Known reuse retains key, immutable bytes, establishing membership and actual origin/attribution/limitations. Preserve separate establishment/creation and local-retention facts, including applicable recorder/owner and grant generation/target. Reject rebinding/recreation and laundering through omitted presentation or unavailable origins, including scope/usage reset; require no global absence proof. Explicit new derivation keeps known attribution. Sufficient retained evidence can avoid an online origin lookup; missing required proof blocks the dependent use. Later provenance discovery adds correction, not silent re-keying or usage reset.
- **Origin selection:** current-grant origin matching means actual local fresh creation under that generation/target, not merely A's retention of B's material.
