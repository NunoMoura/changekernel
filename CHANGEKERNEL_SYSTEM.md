# ChangeKernel System

ChangeKernel preserves owner-controlled pursuits' intent, decisions, evidence and accountable progress across sessions, agents and authorized collaborators. It supports judgment, not a prescribed process.

This document owns desired state, not implementation claims. Proposals and open mechanisms are identified explicitly; design approval authorizes no implementation. [Status](CHANGEKERNEL_STATUS.md) owns gaps and execution until qualified Change operations can; [AGENTS](AGENTS.md) owns procedure; [README](README.md) provides source entrypoints and check commands. History/evidence is not an additional specification.

## Philosophical foundations

This brief research synthesis explains the grounds for the project's concepts. It adopts selected distinctions, not whole philosophical doctrines or proof of software capability. The product contracts below remain explicit; this summary adds no new authority, mechanism or implementation scope.

- **Intent and practical reasons.** [Anscombe](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/anscombe/) connects intentional descriptions with practical reasons; [Bratman's planning account of intention](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/intention/) treats commitments as stable but often partial. These distinctions inform developing intent, WHY/WHAT/HOW and agreement without a complete plan. Observed activity alone establishes neither purpose nor success.
- **Inquiry and interpretation.** [Dewey](https://brocku.ca/MeadProject/Dewey/Dewey_1922/Dewey1922_20.html) treats ends and means as clarified through inquiry; [Gadamer](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/gadamer/) emphasizes contextual, revisable understanding. This informs continued clarification and adaptive approaches, rather than a final interpretation or mandatory experiment cycle. Exact history can preserve grounds without guaranteeing understanding; changed interpretation does not rewrite earlier decisions.
- **Acts, commitments and accountability.** [Speech-act research](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/speech-acts/) and [Brandom's account of commitments and entitlements](https://sites.pitt.edu/~rbrandom/Courses/2023%20Sellars/Sellars%20texts/brandom-making-it-explicit_Chapter%20One.pdf) help distinguish assertions, requests, promises, decisions, undertakings and attribution. This informs attributable operation meanings and the separation of contributions from binding decisions. Wording, inference or a coherent record cannot manufacture another party's authority or commitment.
- **Reasons, uncertainty and learning.** Work on [motivating, explanatory and justificatory reasons](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/reasons-just-vs-expl/), [abduction](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/abduction/peirce.html) and [belief revision](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-belief-revision/) helps distinguish grounds, hypotheses and their reconsideration. It informs the separation of changed beliefs, changed reality and changed commitments. Understanding progress differs from outcome progress; explanation is not truth or endorsement, and retrospective justification supplies no contemporaneous approval.
- **Cooperation and joint commitment.** [Collective-intentionality research](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/collective-intentionality/) distinguishes shared preferences and coordinated activity from joint commitments. This informs distinct personal and collective pursuits, explicit participation and applicable parties' decisions. It supplies no universal voting, veto or delegation policy and no general prohibition on withdrawal.
- **Situated work and autonomy.** The [Suchman](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/fass/doc_library/sociology/Suchman_categories_1994.pdf)–[Winograd](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00749016) debate illuminates both the usefulness and disciplinary risks of formal coordination categories. Alongside work on [autonomy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/autonomy-moral/), it informs contestable interpretation and owner judgment rather than compulsory behavioral taxonomies. Valid approval proves neither informed understanding nor ethical legitimacy; accountable records are not a complete model of human activity.
- **Contextual privacy.** [Nissenbaum's contextual integrity](https://nissenbaum.tech.cornell.edu/papers/Contextual%20Integrity%20Up%20and%20Down.pdf) directs attention to information flows: participants, roles, information types and transmission conditions. This informs separate access and disclosure authority, including the sensitivity of derived information. Reading a Change does not settle redistribution or provider disclosure, and ChangeKernel is not a universal social-norm arbiter.

Together these distinctions inform **Change** as a developing pursuit with accountable history and **Ledger** as its owner's enduring collection. Operations, relations, work units and Views support that pursuit without replacing intent with tasks or prescribing a universal process. These are project design choices informed—not uniquely dictated—by the research.

## Product and boundaries

### Change and Ledger

A **Change** preserves an owner-controlled pursuit's developing intent and history, from one action to months—not a prompt, task list or conversation. Drafting commits nothing; agreement binds exact terms, not a complete plan. Retain alterations, learning/grounds, disproved hypotheses, abandonment and unresolved outcomes. Goals belong within intent.

A **Ledger** is an owner's stable logical collection, not an engine directory. Each person has one across apps, sessions, devices, employer roles and collectives. Agents represent identified owners; sessions create none and selection is explicit. Authorized inclusion establishes a Change's single personal or collective owner, not location. Collectives have independent owners/Ledgers, not unions or writable replicas of personal collections. Identity, representation, inclusion and addressing mechanisms remain open.

**Change and Ledger are the only core primitives.** Queries are interfaces; relations/work units live in histories and indexes/graphs/queues derive from them. Services deploy, but references, participation and versions create no universal State store. A standalone pursuit needs no peer, collective, app, repository or native resource. Roles, phases and tactics are not prescribed; no undeclared semantic veto applies.

### Intent contract: WHY, WHAT, HOW

These are dimensions, not stores, stages, primitives or required fields. Operations develop them; Views project them without one-to-one mappings.

| Dimension | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| WHY | Problems, opportunities, priorities, benefits/tradeoffs, sources and uncertainty. Preserve stated reasons, never invent them; user-endorsed values lead, not observation alone. |
| WHAT | Desired conditions, scope/exclusions, constraints/discretion and evaluation criteria. Artifact requirements bind only if adopted; multiple outcomes may satisfy intent. |
| HOW | Provisional approaches, hypotheses and adaptation grounds. Adopted method constraints bind, but tactics within discretion need no readoption. |

Establish intent, constraints, progress/fulfillment criteria and unknowns. Edits prove no requirements, agreement or improvement; omissions grant no unlimited discretion. Bound uncertainty at affected decisions, not by requiring complete blueprints or blocking unrelated work.

Before undertaking, retain a grounded feasibility, capability, resource, dependency, authority and commitment assessment with limitations/coverage. Missing history proves no absence of conflict; new commitments cannot override old ones silently. Revalidate changed dependent grounds without exhaustive impact detection or whole-Ledger invalidation. Owner adoption and agent undertaking bind identical explicit terms, not provisional context; assessment is neither consent nor a veto over user values.

Pursue fulfillment through inquiry, blockers, adaptation or revision, not response completion. Session loss erases no commitment; unfinished intent expands neither authority nor exemptions from evidence/acceptance and permits no indefinite/background work.

### Understanding, clarification and judgment

Retain decision-relevant understanding, sources, alternatives, assumptions, support/challenge, unknowns and limitations—not private thoughts, compulsory argument graphs or claimed completeness. Owners determine what matters and adopt criteria; agents assess assumptions and propose alternatives. Shared obligations require applicable parties' decisions. Recording must not misstate commitments. ChangeKernel is no universal moral, psychological, communication or social-norm arbiter.

Expression, interpretation, proposal and commitment differ. Records and coherent explanations establish neither truth nor endorsement. Motivating, explanatory and justificatory reasons differ; attribution does not reveal infallible motives. Equal conclusions with different grounds may need different reconsideration. Inference supplies no other's decision/permission, and retrospective explanation supplies no contemporary approval or grounds. Evidence correction, intent revision and execution repair are distinct.

Clarification asks **what is meant**; revision asks **what becomes binding**. Making an always-intended limit explicit need not assert changed intent.

| Situation | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Draft clarification | Elaborate without binding terms |
| Corrected interpretation, fact or explanation | Attributed Contribute with grounds |
| Adaptation within discretion | Contribute without owner readoption |
| Changed supporting grounds/circumstances | Reassess and revalidate affected decisions |
| Changed adopted requirement, criterion, scope or method | Applicable Decide revision |
| Changed permission/disclosure | Authority Decide, not necessarily intent revision |

Labels such as “no change” override no terms. Before relying on unclear effect/permission, retain exact current terms/sources, proposed meaning/effect and applicable participants' attributable explanations or confirmation. Only affected actions wait. The kernel checks subjects, references and authority, not perfect semantic equivalence; interpretation-effect interfaces/authentication remain open.

Contested attribution remains without rewriting decisions. Investigating removal is not approval; failed experiments are not failed Changes. Challenges confer no veto, but authority/integrity failures and changed dependent grounds trigger guards. Successor intent cannot replace revision. Unclear meaning, approach, effects and permission need different responses, not one confidence score. Hypotheses/tests/metrics prove neither truth, consent nor improvement. Learning spends within agreed bounds without a compulsory experiment cycle.

### Progress, completion and readiness

Understanding progress is evidenced improvement in decision-relevant understanding of values, conditions or approaches, including newly discovered uncertainty—not confidence. Outcome progress is evidenced improvement against agreed intent/criteria, not activity or changed targets. Activity/failure may advance either, both or neither; learning outcomes still need evidence/acceptance. Reality, meaning, understanding and commitment prove neither one another, authority nor convergence.

Completion may combine native values, geography, time and owner judgment. Separate fulfillment, optimization and execution limits; identify sources, windows, deadlines and reach-once versus maintenance. Missing evidence is unknown; edits/tasks/proxies prove no native outcome or causation. Flat all-criteria fulfillment is not general. Composition, applicability, temporal evaluation and preauthorized closure remain open; retain [A's settlement safeguards](#adopted-bounded-agreement-profile).

### Ownership and cooperation

Receipt, copying, hosting, sharing and work transfer no ownership. Collective initiation differs from actor identity; later adoption cannot retroactively own a personal Change. Forks preserve distinct pursuits, owners and lineage, not inherited commitments/permissions. Sharing, delivery, delegation, handoff, consolidation and content/responsibility transfer are different acts.

Recipients decide on exact disclosed contributions without another writable source continuation. Copies/restoration/import confer no source authority, adoption or native replay. Multi-party agreements require applicable exact-term decisions; exit erases no responsibility. Personal participation differs from collective representation; outsiders may contribute. App/team membership grants no Change admission, representation, decisions, receipt or action. Voting, administrator, delegation and exit policies remain open; owner withdrawal remains available.

### Resource-scoped participation and native State

Governed inter-Ledger exchange requires native resource scopes and explicit participation terms; direct shared-Change reads do not grant exchange/adoption rights. Apps establish resource identity/authority, not names, paths or URLs alone. Scopes identify sets, namespaces or creation boundaries with subject/version conditions; naming future creation authorizes nothing. Participation binds owners/Ledgers, selected Change interactions, scope, actions, disclosure and authority, not a wire protocol. Participation formats/isolation remain open.

Apps own native State, artifacts, schemas and concurrency and enforce native permissions. ChangeKernel enforces participation, acceptance and disclosure; neither grants the other's authority. References are not universal CRUD or mirrors. Association, relevance or shared agents prove no representation/consent. Bind actor/owner; pool no disclosure or imply private Ledger copying. Changed scope or dependent grounds requires authorization/revalidation; renaming proves no continuity. Unverifiable identity/grounds requires limitation. Purely informational exchange without native resources remains open under existing scoping.

References qualify integration/environment, resource and observed conditions/version. Fresh reads protect no later writes; reports are not independent evidence. App/team/personal versions select exact contributions, results and native subjects, not tips; association, pending work, builds, deployments and app use imply neither Ledger acceptance nor membership. Changes may span releases; upgrades need exact decisions/native evidence. Application contracts govern inclusion, compatibility, retention, support, qualification/recovery, licensing, compensation and maintenance. Audience selection is not isolation; rollback may leave effects. No full-stack framework, package manager, sandbox or marketplace is proposed.

## Authoritative history and content

**Content** is identified payload with provenance, retention and access rules—not necessarily a document or evidence, and not another core primitive. Native artifacts remain references. Retain exact intent/approval source text, adopted terms and required grounds inline or as Change-owned Content without duplication. Hashes, URLs, temporary context, claimed memory and private session links are not retention or continuity. Missing or unverifiable grounds blocks dependent decisions/actions, not unrelated pursuits or healthy originals.

### Record-first kernel and recorded authority

The kernel accepts typed meaning with accountable attribution/authority, exact subjects/versions and relationships, not arbitrary appends. Corrections, revisions, supersession and permission changes append; accepted meaning remains immutable when derived state or SQLite pages change. Independently verifiable receipts need separate design.

Authenticated **Ledger owners** have default authority for supported Ledger operations, waiving no Change policy, retention, acceptance or native constraint. Agents need authenticated representation/delegation; allowlists may restrict it, never create it.

The owner records **initial Change policy**: operation scope, collaborator/agent admission and delegation, Content disclosure, conditions/expiry, amendment and revocation. Current permission derives from those decisions, not hidden ACLs or inferred consent. Standing grants avoid repeated consent, not enforcement. Batches may share checks but never omit final revalidation. Retain authority evidence, not credentials.

Owners and admitted readers have **whole-Change history access**, including future records while authorized—not per-record, type, interval or head ACLs. Filters select context, not rights; historical reads require current permission. Initial policy may grant Content the owner may share. Native actions, Content, provider disclosure, redistribution and other Changes retain separate authority under [contextual privacy](#disclosure-and-contextual-privacy).

Separate owner, represented principal and actual actor; swarm agents remain distinguishable and cannot impersonate owners. Participant evolution changes authority records, not ChangeKeys. General policy/delegation schemas, bootstrap, identity/agent binding and A's versioned replacement remain open; never silently widen old grants/identities. Queries append no pursuit records; auditing waives no required host recording.

### Acceptance invariants

Bind exact actions, inputs, intent, requirements and grounds; revalidate scope, concurrent preconditions and current authority, never caller approval flags. Exact retries recover original results; conflicting reuse fails. Timeout, lost reply, cancellation and disconnect prove neither nonacceptance nor rollback. Observation, assessment, producer completion, recipient acceptance and fulfillment differ. Withdrawal stops future pursuit, not accountability, and implies no cancellation, rollback or fulfillment. Recovery needs current authority; documentation/codecs/retries cannot reinterpret history or revive superseded authority.

Kernel guarantees and user requirements share evidence/evaluation semantics, never authority. Extensions cannot redefine operations, waive guarantees or confer permission; advisory packs activate nothing. Separate definition, adoption, applicability, evaluation and assessment. Begin with bounded structural, reference, range, decision and review requirements, not scripts. Evidence binds exact subject/requirement versions, reviewer/profile, inputs, judgment, uncertainty and applicability grounds; missing, stale, unsupported or inconclusive evidence cannot pass.

Evaluators require separate authority/budgets outside kernel/custody. No circular/unbounded evaluation or reinterpretation through reruns; schemas, lifecycle and qualification remain open without a model dependency. Human approval holds no global admission/database lock: prepare exact subjects, then revalidate at acceptance. Reservation protocols remain open.

## Composable reads and Views

The complete elementary surface covers Ledger discovery, Change records, retained Content and native references. Families are capabilities, not a fixed method count; P34 is a delivery subset. Agents compose ordinary host-side code such as JavaScript/codemode, not an additional composition framework or prescribed workflow. Observe quality/cost before adding higher-level queries; installation requires separate authority.

`ReadReply<T>` distinguishes `Returned` and `Refused`; transport failure is neither a valid refusal nor empty data. Bind qualified sources, boundaries/native versions, definition/selection, current permission, exact references and coverage without mandatory request echoes or duplicate inventories. Public history preserves accepted meaning, attribution, decisions, grant terms, outcomes and necessary source facts, not raw `StoredOperation`, private `Checks` or Content bytes. Summaries replace no sources/decisions; distinguish contemporary and retrospective explanation. Reads grant no write/export/backup authority; private files are not APIs. Denial reveals no protected sizes, hashes or explanatory details.

### Read families and composition

| Call | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| `ReadLedger` | `ledger`, `selection`, optional `pageCursor`, `limits`. Search recording sources in that Ledger. `AllDiscoverable` returns permitted members; `HasRecord(RecordMatch)` returns matching members; `HasRecordedRelation(RelationMatch)` returns permitted related references grounded in searched records, including foreign Changes. |
| `ReadChange(ReadChangeRequest) → ReadChangeReply` | `change: ChangeKey`, fixed `range: {after, through}`, `AllRecords` or `MatchingRecords(RecordMatch)`, optional `pageCursor`, `limits`. Return public records, actual scanned interval, observed head and `More {nextPageCursor}` or `EndOfRange`, not current-state projections or Content/native bytes. |
| `ResolveChangeCursor` | `change`, `anchor`, `limits`. Resolve Beginning, Current, Operation or Sequence to an exact position; invalid anchors refuse, never clamp. |
| `ReadContent` | P34: `change`, one exact accepted Content membership and `limits`. Return exact retained bytes or refusal, without catalog enumeration, automatic chunking or native fetch. |

Qualified keys, cursors and references pass between calls without reinterpretation. References establish no target membership, availability or access. Never fetch target titles/history implicitly; search other Ledgers and retrieve earlier grounds explicitly under current authority. Content retrieval requires current Content/source authority and distinguishes not requested, reason-free denial, permitted unavailability and bytes.

**RecordMatch** has optional sets `kinds: Set<OperationKind>`, `operations: Set<OperationRef>`, `actors: Set<ActorRef>` and `references: Set<QualifiedReference>`. Fields match the same accepted record; values within each field are alternatives. Actors are actual recorded actors, not represented owners. Identities/references are qualified, not names, URLs or ambiguous local IDs. Use explicit all-selection instead of an empty matcher; refuse unsupported predicates. Matching a reference retrieves no target bytes.

**RelationMatch** has qualified `endpoint: ChangeKey`, optional `kind` and `direction: Incoming | Outgoing | Either` relative to that endpoint. A one-hop recorded link is neither similarity nor counterpart endorsement: A's link to B need not appear in B. Further traversal is agent composition requiring target permission. Text/content search and semantic similarity remain separate open contracts.

**Ledger pages:** `ReadLedgerReply = ReadReply<LedgerPage>` distinguishes matches, source observations, progress and coverage; layout remains open. Emit each qualified Change key once per query, with its first matching-record reference for record/relation selections—not every match, truth or current effectiveness. Page replay may repeat entries. Reuse `RecordMatch` and the observed boundary in `ReadChange`; relation grounds/cursors belong to the recording Change. Give permitted evaluation boundaries once per source per page, including evaluated nonmatches; discovery invents no history observation. Progress is `More {nextPageCursor}` or `EndOfScan`, not completeness.

Traverse sources in qualified-key order and records in acceptance order, not global target-key, time or importance ranking. Fix a source's boundary when first examined; later appends require another scan. Recheck current permission. Changed permitted membership invalidates continuation: safely refuse for restart, without leaking hidden-only changes. Earlier pages remain observations, not coverage of the changed collection. No automatic restart loops; busy collections may repeatedly invalidate scans. Bounded uniqueness and private membership/cursor mechanisms remain open.

Indexes and graphs provide candidates, not authority or verified originals. Hide member metadata/counts not authorized for disclosure. Collection progress is neither a Change position nor a global snapshot. Missing/unverified membership or sources leaves gaps, not nonmatches; healthy originals remain usable. Bound depth, fan-out and total work.

**Native retrieval:** proposed `ReadReferencedArtifact` resolves recorded integration/environment, resource and version/conditions through a trusted adapter, not URLs alone or silent latest substitution. History boundaries freeze no native artifact. Return permitted source/version/coverage; distinguish denial, unavailability and changed sources. Apply current Change authority, needed delegation, native access, disclosure and credential custody under [shared app authorization](#shared-app-authorization), retaining H admission/outcome recording. Retrieval neither creates Content automatically nor waives required-ground retention.

Standing scope needs no per-composition approval; wrapping grants no authority. Trusted boundaries enforce authority, disclosure and aggregate work below scripts. No arbitrary SQL/private-store API or JavaScript belongs in the kernel. Minimize repetition without losing meaning, grounds or limitations; preserve input progress and distinguish input coverage from output completeness.

Only qualified, versioned projections carry defined determinism/coverage guarantees. Equal qualified sources, boundaries/native versions, selection, permission/availability and definition yield equal content; qualify head/coverage observations separately. Ordinary transformations inherit neither guarantee nor authority/lifecycle meaning. Current applicability follows explicit statuses and relationships, not old prose.

### Ranges, coverage and resource bounds

Positions are logical bookmarks in a qualified original/profile, never physical offsets. Beginning is zero; other positions validate sequence/OperationId. Resolve anchors once and fix `(after=a, through=n]`, `0 <= a <= n`; appends never move the endpoints. Use acceptance order, not random IDs. “Cut” is not a public read type; private persistence terminology is separate.

A Change `pageCursor` binds source/profile, fixed range, selection/definition, reader/permission and last scanned position, not last match. `nextPageCursor` advances that scan. Empty filtered pages may advance and all-filtered ranges may finish; verified pages may stop at a normal page limit with `More`. Cursors are not owned entities, shared mutable read positions, authority or consumption evidence. Resume/restart revalidates the original and current permission.

Coverage distinguishes scans, matches, filter skips, omissions and observed heads. Keep eligibility/head/lag unknowns explicit; disclose missing, unavailable, stale or unsupported grounds only when permitted. Contiguous verified pages establish selected-range coverage, not complete context or an integrity audit. Cursor absence alone proves no completeness. Stateful composition needs earlier binding grounds; a match-free interval rules out no obligations. Mixed source positions/versions imply no clock-derived causality; changed scope/filter needs renewed coverage. Recover missed notifications from retained history, advance derived updates/progress together and deduplicate. Publish valid generations or explicit partial coverage, never unexplained gaps or half-rebuilt state as complete.

**Resource bounds apply before work:** copying, decoding, verification, reconstruction and allocation, plus scans, entries and complete encoded replies, including envelopes, nested lengths, coverage, omissions and cursors. Bound native calls/bytes/processing, constituent reads and total composition—not just printed output. Preserve nested bounds and full P33 verification; pagination does not bound cold entry or current-authority reconstruction. No ordinary-acceptance ceiling, history truncation or unbounded fallback. An oversized first item refuses without skipping, truncating or advancing past it. Source-limit refusal proves no completeness; errors disclose only permitted facts. Justify numeric limits and historical availability with measurements plus work/allocation analysis; a timeout/output cap alone is insufficient. Requested limits only tighten enforced bounds.

Queries/rebuilds invoke no native actions; explicit native retrieval is a governed host read. Projection/notification failures block no unrelated acceptance, and readiness indexes replace no admission checks. No bus, per-Change poller or session transport is required. Delivery proves no understanding, authority, retention, recovery or native freshness; interpretation uncertainty and host delivery/refresh remain explicit.

### P34 first delivery boundary

**Adopted local-owner profile:** position resolution, history and retained Content for one included local original under existing owner-role checks and supplied trust—not an authenticated service. Require separate history/Content disclosure from the trusted boundary; inherit no broad Change-to-Content fallback or old action-grant history rights. Invent no identity/authentication evidence, owner impersonation or general collaboration/delegation.

P34 cursors are validated stateless bookmarks carrying only permitted data, not signed receipts. Validate all claimed positions/bindings; constructing one grants no access or proof of earlier scanning. Each reply establishes only actual scanned coverage. No progress/cursor tables or automatic retry loops. This selects no Ledger cursor privacy mechanism.

Public records carry semantic Command/Accepted, used grant, membership/intake and provenance, discretion/native evidence and actual ground-version observations, excluding private controls and payloads. Local entry starts from an Included reference, not an activated writer; bounded physical preparation precedes fresh read-only verification of pairs, replay, normalized controls and the exact inclusion receipt. Supplied callbacks must be bounded, non-fetching and serialized with context changes; this is a trust precondition, not a callback sandbox.

Use existing `prost`: read protocol **2**, request/reply magic `CKRC`/`CKRR`, then big-endian u32 version and body length, then strict Protobuf. `ReadCall` selects Resolve, Change or exact Content; `ReadReply<ReadValue>` returns the corresponding Position, Change page or bytes, or a reason-only refusal. Complete call/reply maxima are **64 KiB / 2 MiB**. Stored `(3,3,3)` and its tags remain unchanged; no new dependency, native feature or migration is selected. Preserve final current-permission/source/custody/head checks even for earlier history. Failed calls release no partial page or older-storage fallback; denied history is not empty. Never increase a caller's ceiling; inability to fit a complete refusal is codec failure.

Per-page limits are **1–256 scanned**, **1–64 returned**, **64 bytes–2 MiB reply**, defaulting to **32 / 16 / 1 MiB**. Cold preflight admits at most **256 accepted records**. Positive, separately supplied per-call work ceilings may only tighten **64 MiB observed source lengths**, **1 GiB reserved I/O**, **2 GiB cumulative allocation** and **2,147,483,648 work units**. These structural reservations are not RSS/elapsed-time limits or availability guarantees; valid originals may conservatively refuse. Ordinary acceptance limits are unchanged.

**Approved Serde deferral:** P34 public read types need not implement generic Serde serialization/deserialization for this delivery. Keep the reviewed Protobuf interface; revisit Serde when a concrete integration needs it. The two can coexist. This is a narrow exception to C-SERDE, not other Rust/API, validation, permission or resource requirements.

Exclude rollups, caches, aggregation, subscriptions, Ledger discovery, foreign or automatic out-of-range traversal, native retrieval and export/transport/host/swarm integration. Later collaboration need not block local owner reads. Implementation/integration follows the [bounded-cycle requirements](AGENTS.md#workflow); changes to product contracts, trust boundaries, dependencies, stored formats or agreed scope require renewed approval. General policy/bootstrap/agent binding, Ledger mechanisms, added predicates/recursive filters/search, broader batching/failures and native/connector/H qualification remain open.

## Change operations

These semantic families are not APIs, phases or interchangeable payloads. Interfaces may combine calls, not their effects/preconditions. Clarification, learning and explanation need no extra families. All obey [recorded authority and acceptance](#record-first-kernel-and-recorded-authority).

| Family | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Authorized initiation opens/includes a draft, not agreement/pursuit permission. |
| Contribute | Attributed proposals, questions, interpretations, plans, observations, hypotheses, findings, corrections, explanations, reviews, relations, adaptations and assessments. Retain sources, limitations, benefits/harms and uncertainty; adopt nothing. |
| Decide | Exact-subject adoption, undertaking, rejection, revision, retraction, reaffirmation, authority or settlement under the applicable rule. |
| Admit action | Record admission/denial against current intent, authority, grounds and native conditions before dispatch, not execution/success. |
| Report outcome | Sourced results, failures, uncertainty and original-action reconciliation, not replay/reopening. |

Investigation is an authorized host action, not a pre-commit phase. Contributions are neither private reasoning nor consent/host outcomes. Managed View queries require host recording without mutating accepted history.

### Related Changes

Separate pursuits when agreement, ownership, authority, acceptance or lifecycle usefully differ, not per task/entity/experiment; prefer reuse over mandatory decomposition trees. Relations retain type, exact endpoints/subjects, grounds and uncertainty. A's claims impose no B decision/delivery obligation. Dependencies may concern outputs rather than whole-Change completion. Allow feedback, not impossible strict-prerequisite cycles; global acyclicity and optimal decomposition are not promised.

Authorized Ledger queries/Views support comparison; similarity adopts no relationship. Starting, forking, splitting, consolidating or replacing uses Start, Contribute and applicable Decide, preserving lineage, disclosed subjects and separate histories/responsibilities. Context and reused grounds create no obligation, adoption or source authority. Support/challenge/benefit claims concern particular subjects under the judgment rules. Ending commitments requires Decide, not just claim retraction.

Dependencies need Contribute plus Decide: “requires” conditions a named action; fulfillment dependencies need adopted terms. Keep exact versions and proposal status. Changed grounds/conflicts require impact/tradeoff records and applicable reaffirmation, revision, allocation or prioritization, never silent precedence. One initiating history admits/reports shared execution; each Change assesses consequences separately without pooled grants or automatic fulfillment. Missing grounds may remain inconclusive; supporting success proves no other goal and abandoned tactics need not defeat intent. Exact lifecycle, concurrent dependency/cycle validation and recovery remain open.

### Work units and execution

Optional bounded units organize execution within a Change, not separately owned intent. Separate preparation, resource allocation and dispatch. Briefs retain intent, requested result/inquiry, inputs, capabilities, dependencies, authority, limits and evidence expectations, not exhaustive phases. Unit completion is not fulfillment; failed attempts need not abandon it.

Contribute prepares/revises; Views derive eligibility; authorized Decide/host coordination allocates, prioritizes and assigns. Queues are not admission. Claim/dispatch binds unit/attempt/worker, prevents accidental duplicates and revalidates managed actions through Admit. Outcomes differ from quality and acceptance. Pause, cancellation, reassignment and retry require current authority/grounds and applicable decisions, not inferred rollback.

ChangeKernel retains associations, requests, decisions, limits and outcomes; hosts execute. Capacity waits/priority authorize neither dispatch nor cancellation. Budgets cannot multiply across Changes, units or attempts. Expiry/loss proves no absence of effects; session end closes no unit or Change. Preserve original-attempt accountability and revalidate queued dispatch. Identity/claims, lifecycle/accounting and partial-result recovery remain open. No capacity automation, universal active-Change quota or maximum-efficiency guarantee: bound active work, not history, learning or responsibility.

## Adopted bounded agreement profile

**A** is the bounded one-owner/one-undertaking-agent agreement profile, not the general identity/delegation model. Its rules remain until an approved versioned replacement; owner defaults and read policy do not reinterpret it. **H** supplies authentic caller/approval binding, native scope and dispatch; **D** supplies durable identity and retention.

- **Subjects:** proposals bind owner/Ledger/Change, expected agreement or absence, exact terms, context and unknowns. Decisions name complete immutable subjects, actor/represented owner, authority and versioned dependencies. Equal text or moving tips are not identity. Undertaking names its retained assessment and limitations/coverage.
- **Agreement:** either decision order is allowed. The second matching decision activates only while both remain effective, the predecessor current and the Change nonterminal. Unmatched decisions may retract. Revision uses the same rule; proposals neither replace nor suspend terms. Reject stale/mismatched activation and preserve supersession.
- **Changed grounds:** stale only explicitly dependent decisions; affected parties must reaffirm before activation/admission. Agents cannot renew owner decisions. Changed terms need matching new decisions and never restore a superseded predecessor.
- **Authority:** owners control adoption/revision, grants, fulfillment and withdrawal; agents undertake for their identified role. Grants target named drafts/bounded draft creation for recording/disclosure, exact agreements for pursuit, or original actions for reconciliation. Draft grants cannot dispatch models, tools or native actions, investigate, disclose externally or bootstrap pre-agreement activity.
- **Grants:** name recipient, target, actions, disclosure/native scope and limits; omissions grant nothing. Generation begins at one; replacement/revocation requires and advances the expected generation. Revoked IDs are not reusable. Each operation needs one complete grant, without union or revision inheritance; new grants cannot revive queued old generations.
- **Dispatch:** require current agreement/grant generation, scope, retained grounds and supported native conditions. Revision, revocation and settlement invalidate queued permissions; H revalidates at dispatch. Dispatched actions retain their original agreement/grant association.
- **Readiness/progress:** drafts may lack terms/criteria. Agreement needs nonempty binding terms, not exhaustive answers or criteria; undertaking needs the assessment. Progress names current agreement, affected terms, optional applicable criteria, evidence and both progress dimensions, not fulfillment. Adaptation names agreement, grounds and approach.
- **Settlement:** fulfillment requires current agreement, criteria/evidence and resolved relevant blockers; withdrawal needs neither success nor resolved actions. Both stop pursuit, late activation/revision, queued permission and reopening. Separately authorized accountability retains original-action association and uncertainty without replay.

### Adopted extension to A: agreement references and roles

Content membership belongs to an accepted operation in the same owner/Ledger/Change. Reject missing, spliced, foreign-operation, moving or rebound memberships. Only the current operation's own bindings may be provisional, validated atomically. Membership grants no authorship, storage or permission; attribution, access and retention still apply.

Use finite explicit binding/context selections, criteria and questions, not dimension tags. Selected terms/criteria bind. Binding terms and criterion definitions cannot also be context, even through aliases. Mixed prose requires an explicit retained proposed-term statement linked to its source, not silent extraction. Decisions bind the complete immutable proposal and roles; context may interpret under clarification rules but cannot weaken requirements or imply inherited selections.

Criteria freeze their definition and nonempty exact links to binding terms; changes need fresh identity. Reuse transfers no terms or fulfillment, and evidence/acceptance names current agreement and criterion. Contributions/questions may link affected subjects; proposal edits need fresh IDs and agreed-term/criterion/role changes need matching revision decisions. Context links resolve no question or replace terms. Durable schema remains open.

### Adopted extension to A: future-content recording

One current recording grant may cover fresh contributions/sources without per-text renewal. It binds recipient, owner/Ledger/Change, draft/agreement/original-action target, internal operations and whole-operation fresh same-record bindings. It permits no prior ingestion, grant union, Ledger wildcard or separate Content-creation API; A's draft restrictions remain.

Atomically retain bytes, recorder/owner, attribution and origin—grant/generation/target plus creating operation/content—with usage and record. Origin is not authorship/originality; provisional origins authorize nothing. Apply qualified intake and exact-retry rules below. Grants may select future accepted creations under their own generation/target; other inputs require explicit scope. New/replacement grants inherit nothing but may select exact IDs or historical origins. Revocation preserves origin, retention and independently authorized accountability without cascading revocation or revived queues.

Creation/use does not authorize disclosure, sharing, export, native fetch or model calls. Current disclosure may separately select accepted origins; source authority and H authentication remain required. Settlement permits only separately authorized original-action reconciliation writes, not resumed pursuit. Per-operation bounds apply. Optional cumulative quotas, if adopted, charge logical bytes/items for every accepted new identity, including equal bytes, but not references/retries; usage survives generations and cap replacement. Revalidate scope, generation, limits, sources and disclosure at acceptance; exhaustion blocks creation, not retention. Qualify H/D enforcement and prove same-record creation and return/reuse under one initial grant, including authority, limit and retry failures.

## Durable identity and storage

### Adopted storage architecture

One owner-local SQLite original per Change atomically holds records/results, required Content/origins/grounds and acceptance controls. Ledger membership/custody evidence is authoritative, not a View. Ordinary acceptance needs no other Change/View commit; actual dependencies and native conditions still apply. Accepted Change permission records and derived policy remain inside; host/native state, credentials and external authority remain outside.

Authority layout, client stores, projections and transport are separate. Authorized provisional saves are neither acceptance nor another writable continuation. No paid service is mandatory. Targets: **Rust 1.99.1, edition 2021; SQLite 3.54.1**, subject to qualification.

### Adopted independent-Change profile

Qualify Operation, Proposal, Agreement, Assessment, Decision, Grant, Action and Criterion by owner/Ledger/Change and check collisions there. Change IDs are unique within their owner-bound Ledger. Owner, Ledger, Actor, Ground, GroundVersion, Scope, Limit, NativeAction and ContextVersion retain issuing scopes; Action is not NativeAction. Qualify nested subjects, predecessors, equality and targets; values, files and routes establish no identity.

Missing members are not empty; surviving-file scans prove no complete Ledger. Distinguish lost discovery, incomplete collection and invalid originals. Fence affected originals without skipping damage or promoting copies. Collection backups declare membership coverage and individual history boundaries, not global consistency. Foreign origin admissibility and View protocols remain gates.

### Adopted trusted-port durable records

D preserves A and history. Rules/IDs/records are OS-independent; Linux is first and other platforms require adapter/build/storage tests. This slice excludes H, app access, multi-agent execution and general Views/retrieval.

- **IDs:** typed nonzero 16-byte values survive retry/restart/restoration without conveying authority/native identity. OS-secure adapter randomness generates candidates; fail on entropy errors and bound zero resampling, without clock/counter fallback or an allocator service. Reuse established actor/owner/Ledger identities. Atomically distinguish creation collisions from references/retries; qualify host retention reuse, not another journal.
- **Build:** pin `rusqlite` 0.41.0, sys 0.39.0 and `prost` 0.14.4; review locks/licenses. Disable rusqlite defaults and enable only `backup`/`limits`; prost uses `derive`/`std`, checked-in definitions and no protoc. Statically link externally built, checksum-verified official SQLite 3.54.1, not bundled SQLite. Require explicit include/library paths, thread safety, disabled extension loading/double-quoted strings, no pkg-config/ambient fallback or Cargo-time downloads, and runtime version/source-ID/options checks. Preparation needs authorization; [README](README.md#controlled-source-checks-linux) owns commands and operational guards.
- **Records:** retain original request/result, identity/predecessor, checks, grant use/snapshot and required Content/origins/grounds. Restoration validates ordered linkage, results, bindings, origins, controls and earlier-record references under supported interpretation, not fresh execution. Missing/damaged grounds block writable opening; refuse unsupported profiles before mode mutation without skipping or repairing originals.
- **Acceptance:** apply [acceptance invariants](#acceptance-invariants) and the request/format rules below. Use local WAL, verified `synchronous=FULL`, foreign keys/constraints, distinct creation/opening and bound identity. Hold local guards through the short complete-record transaction; publish memory only on known acceptance. Fence uncertain handles; reopen/validate the same original to resolve its ID. Only established noncommit permits unchanged resubmission.
- **Custody:** trusted private local files and an exclusive lifetime writer lease distinct from SQL locks. Refuse competing writers, wrong identity, detectable replacement/corruption and unsupported profiles/filesystems. Use short snapshots, immediate busy refusal only for established noncommit and bounded passive checkpoints. Denial/fencing permits noncheckpointing release; maintenance cannot undo acceptance. Temporary storage is unsuitable; qualify identity, locking, durability, permissions and lifecycle.
- **Backup:** owner-default or explicit scoped authority plus destination/disclosure permission, never history access alone. Use quiesced SQLite backup, not live-file copying; preserve original bytes/versions/grounds or disclose omissions, excluding unrelated private Content. Verify completeness and sync snapshot/destination directory before success. Copies permit read-only reconstruction, not writable activation or replay. The format rules govern upgrades; normal original restart remains required. Never invent empty Changes or collection coverage.

### Adopted qualified representation and current development format

**Keys:** `ChangeKey = (OwnerId, LedgerId, ChangeId)`; `ContentKey = (establishing ChangeKey, ContentId)`. Bound containers may qualify local leaves/predecessors, not replace Identity/participant/authority checks. Use full keys in maps, grants/disclosure, origins, claims, retained sets, references, roles and retry equality. `(B,x)` differs from `(C,x)`; aliases cannot evade role exclusivity. A retains foreign grounds through exact `(IdentityA, local OperationId, ContentKey)` membership, never latest presence or a foreign operation pointer. Foreign establishment evidence cannot substitute for A's membership.

**Intake:** trusted validation distinguishes fresh, first retention and known reuse, not agent flags. Fresh or pre-existing unbound input establishes only in the accepting Change. First retention records provenance/kind/uncertainty, not creation/authorship. Known reuse preserves key, immutable bytes, establishing membership, origin, attribution and limitations. Separate establishment/creation from local retention, including recorder/owner/grant generation/target. Reject rebinding, recreation, omitted/unavailable-origin laundering and scope/usage resets without requiring global absence proof. Derivations retain known attribution. Sufficient retained evidence may avoid online origin lookup; missing proof blocks dependent use. Later provenance discovery adds correction, not re-keying.

**Origins:** current-grant selection means local fresh creation under its generation/target, not retention of another Change's Content. Historical-origin selection needs validated qualified creation facts retained through A's membership and current A authority. First retention fabricates no fresh origin. No new retention selector or inherited foreign authority; explicit qualified selections remain available.

**OperationRequest:** durably retain profile, target ChangeKey, ActorId, represented OwnerId, complete typed Operation, creation IDs and original bytes/versions before submission. Lookup is `(ChangeKey, OperationId)`; actor/profile changes conflict, not create namespaces. Check original/nested scopes, including legitimate foreign keys, against current authenticated actor/owner. Declarations grant nothing. Preserve original outcomes and current source/disclosure checks; never infer targets from routes, retarget uncertainty, regenerate IDs or replay native work.

**Local intake:** support fresh creation, declared first retention and locally established reuse. Derivation inputs name exact earlier local membership under current source/Content authority; known inputs cannot become fresh. First retention needs trusted pre-existing/no-known-binding observations with attribution/limitations/uncertainty, without fresh origin or creation charge. Bind actual authorized inputs/conditions independently of labels/candidate keys and revalidate at acceptance. Local accepted-prefix facts establish identity, but fixtures authenticate no native source. Represent foreign keys; refuse foreign establishment/origin acceptance until evidence/custody is qualified. Separate original establishment facts from later knowledge; retries need not reproduce pre-establishment observations. Missing proof/declarations, contradictions or changed immutable facts block use, not future authorized foreign reuse.

**Encoding and format:** length-framed Protobuf retains exact bytes and codec/schema/semantic versions, not Rust-only encoding or required gRPC. Support one current tuple `(3,3,3)`, an internal version, not a release or maintained earlier-development reader. Incompatible layout/meaning needs explicit advancement. Before payload preflight/allocation/decode validate the bounded envelope and reject old, missing, unknown or inconsistent versions unaltered. Enforce 16 MiB records, 1 MiB/item, 8 MiB/64 fresh Content items/operation, 4,096 entries/repeated field and depth 32; tighter grants apply, not cumulative quotas.

Reject malformed frames, unknown fields/enums, duplicate singular/oneof fields, missing meaningful presence, invalid IDs and duplicate set members; preserve list order. Never reuse retired tags, default meaning or ignore unsupported constraints. Review mapping before implementation. Preserve original bytes and behavioral evidence at their revision, not frozen legacy implementations. Re-encoding, hashes and noncanonical serialization establish no historical identity, authority or exactly-once effects; decoding alone proves no valid history/outcome.

No silent conversion/re-keying/default qualifiers, known-to-unbound relabeling, mixed profiles, live replacement, writable-copy activation, merge/import, migration/downgrade or automatic deletion. Adopt retention/compatibility policy before promising user-history access across format changes.

### Adopted local persistence slice

On Linux x86_64 GNU/Btrfs, demonstrate owner-controlled Start/local intake, guarded acceptance and same-original restart/outcome recovery for two independent Changes. Other A in-process bootstrap behavior is unchanged.

- **Inclusion:** one protected owner/Ledger SQLite register reserves complete retained creation requests/ChangeKeys and binds allocated originals before Start acceptance. Confirm inclusion and retain a request/issuer/custody-bound receipt before ordinary writable activation. These recoverable commits are not cross-database atomicity; pending inclusion never undoes Start. Reconcile the original request without replacement IDs/originals and preserve unexplained artifacts. Caller retention is supplied trust, not a host journal.
- **Independence:** the register handles creation/reconciliation, not ordinary acceptance or heads. Known originals use retained receipts and valid custody/current authority without live discovery. Register loss blocks new creation, inclusion recovery and complete collection claims, not healthy originals. Exclude membership removal, custody rebinding, multi-device creation and competing registers.
- **Commit cuts:** outside WAL, retain a verified cut and one pending mutation per store, not another history. Publish Prepared before SQL mutation and CommitPossible durably before COMMIT; finalize the after-cut before success. Prepared plus verified before-cut proves noncommit; CommitPossible plus exact after-cut recovers acceptance, while a miss stays indeterminate. No automatic abort/resubmission or collection commit for ordinary operations.
- **Restart:** outside WAL-dependent state retain custody/profile-bound lifecycle evidence: live before acceptance with recorded journal objects, closing with a verified cut before cleanup. Publish clean only after quiescent checkpoint coverage, complete close/sync and main-database head/integrity verification against that cut, holding lease/current maintenance guard throughout. Interrupted closing requires the same proof; WAL absence is insufficient. Missing WAL cannot expose an older prefix as current or prove noncommit. Preserve sidecar pins and safe noncheckpointing release.
- **Preflight:** exclusively quiesce custody and verify originals through private read-only copies before potentially mutating SQLite access. Check source objects throughout copying and require original lifecycle/journal evidence; copies are neither writers nor backups. Retain exact request/record byte pairs and cross-check ordered prefix/head and normalized controls. Redundancy costs storage and detects disagreement, not coherent forgery, rollback or authentic provenance; no new digest/dependency.

Disable automatic close checkpointing; guarded PASSIVE plus main-only cut verification establishes clean close. Block/preserve ambiguous allocation remnants. Review publication, encoding and connection flags before edits. Source/caller trust is supplied; foreign origins are unavailable. Export/backup, Views, H, migration and controller handoff remain excluded; runtime/power-loss claims need qualification.

## Harness and service

### Host boundary

ChangeKernel targets a human-centered interoperable standard across harnesses/vendors without owning models or runtimes. **Pi Durable (`@earendil-works/pi-durable`) is first, not exclusive:** experimental and distinct from ordinary Pi. Revision, storage/profile, hooks, interception, gateways and interoperability remain open; participation is not governed execution.

Typed operations and elementary queries share a service contract through thin code-mode adapters; no new CLI. Hosts own sessions, agents, tools, context, processes, plugins, scheduling, general memory and providers. The independent owner service owns acceptance, grounds and disclosure; native/OS systems enforce access/confinement.

Agents call typed read tools and receive their defined structured results. Backend/host code handles wire decoding and value mapping, preserving references, coverage, grounds and refusals—not silently summarizing them away. Agents choose follow-up calls or explanations; they need not convert Protobuf. The [fixture-only adapter core](#fixture-only-read-adapter-core) is the bounded conversion delivery, not a live host adapter.

Host attempts differ from semantic operations and durable records. Below scripts bind exact intent/authority/actor/owner, durably Admit before dispatch, source outcomes/uncertainty and reconcile originals. Independent commits provide neither service/native atomicity nor complete delivery. Nested tools, shells, extensions, subprocesses and delegation require bypass control, not observation. Hidden activity and missing grounds remain uncovered; private host state is not accepted history. Correlation, custody, capture and recovery remain open.

### Fixture-only read-adapter core

**Adopted bounded delivery boundary:** backend projection, request validation and a fixture-backed call harness for ResolveChangeCursor, ReadChange and ReadContent over one Included local original. Reuse the [P34 public types, codec and read entry](#p34-first-delivery-boundary), not another read engine. This core demonstrates conversion under supplied fixture trust, not live agent access, authenticated service behavior or H qualification.

Backend software handles Protobuf conversion and produces faithful structured values. Preserve qualified references, accepted meaning, attribution/provenance, actual ground observations, fixed ranges, actual scanned coverage, observed heads and continuation. Preserve exact Content, including empty and non-text bytes; identifiers, integers and variants must remain lossless. Match reply kind to the requested call. Private acceptance controls, context tokens, custody paths and credentials are not public fields. Keep semantic refusals distinct from empty success and from transport/codec/adapter failures; denial reveals no protected details.

Apply bounds before decoding, projection, allocation and complete output construction, including encoding expansion, envelopes and copies. Adapter limits do not widen caller or P34 ceilings. Fail rather than deliver a truncated or partial result. No silent summaries, automatic paging/chunking, retries, cached fallback, implicit traversal, native fetching, Ledger discovery or new CLI. Routine layout and numeric choices may be settled within an explicitly authorized bounded cycle, with justified enforced limits and behavioral/failure tests before integration.

For this fixture delivery, named JSON records are objects, not positional arrays; variants remain explicit. IDs use lowercase hexadecimal, wide integers use canonical unsigned decimal strings, and exact bytes use tagged UTF-8 or hexadecimal without normalization. Enforce **32 KiB input JSON and accepted reply frames**, **128 KiB backend construction/complete compact JSON output**, **8,192 projection nodes** and **16 MiB projection allocation**. Total adapter reservations are at most **1,536 MiB allocation / 64 Mi work units**, charged against caller work before the backend receives the remainder. These are conservative cumulative reservations, not RSS guarantees; combined maxima may not fit, and larger valid P34 results may fail with an adapter limit error. Preserve original-limit validation before tightening. This selects neither a production transport nor a stable host API.

Trusted fixture wiring supplies the Included reference, current owner/actor observation, separate history/Content permissions and custody/verification location. Agent-facing selectors establish none of these. Preserve P34's owner-actor restriction; invent no owner impersonation or delegated-reader authority. Production transport and authentic owner-mediated disclosure versus general agent reading remain approval gates, not fixture conclusions.

For this core's structured JSON conversion, use `serde = 1.0.229` with `std`/`derive` and `serde_json = 1.0.154` with `std`, both exactly pinned with defaults disabled. Review the necessary locked transitive graph, source, licenses and resolved features before integration; preserve existing dependency versions/features. Reconcile the strict dependency-lock/feature checks with the approved graph without relaxing them or changing native build behavior. Keep conversion in the adapter: no blanket domain derives, private-control serialization or extension of P34's Serde exception. Read protocol 2 and stored `(3,3,3)` remain unchanged.

Live integration additionally needs a selected immutable host/runtime/dependency and staging profile, faithful delivery through the chosen tool/code-mode channels, and the [host-boundary](#host-boundary), [recovery](#inquiry-binding-and-recovery) and [privacy](#disclosure-and-contextual-privacy) guarantees. Structured output alone proves neither model delivery nor retained grounds. Further dependency, trust, transport or broader scope changes require approval before implementation.
